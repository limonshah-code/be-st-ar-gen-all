Historically, the Apple Head type has been considered the "classic" Chihuahua look. This sometimes leads to higher demand for them. Breeders who focus on AKC standards might charge more for puppies that closely match the Apple Head ideal. This is not always the case, however.

### Common Health Concerns

Despite their robustness, Apple Head Chihuahuas have specific health considerations. Their unique head shape can make them prone to certain conditions. One concern is hydrocephalus, a buildup of fluid in the brain. They can also have dental issues due to their small mouths. Patellar luxation, a slipping kneecap, is also common in small breeds. Hypoglycemia, low blood sugar, can affect puppies. Responsible owners monitor for these issues.

### Investing in Preventative Care

Preventative care is key to their long-term health. Regular vet check-ups catch problems early. A balanced diet supports their immune system. Regular dental care prevents gum disease. Proper exercise keeps them at a healthy weight. These measures can prevent costly treatments later. Investing in good care helps your Chihuahua live a full life.
