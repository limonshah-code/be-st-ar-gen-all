**Key Takeaways for Cane Corso Owners and Enthusiasts:**
*   Cane Corsos are not illegal nationwide in the US.
*   Local jurisdictions, not federal law, implement breed-specific legislation (BSL).
*   Research your specific city or county laws before acquiring a Cane Corso.
*   Responsible ownership, training, and socialization are crucial for all powerful breeds.
*   Insurance policies may have restrictions based on breed.

Cane Corsos are not illegal across the entire United States. No federal law bans this breed. Instead, restrictions or outright bans on Cane Corsos exist at the local level. These vary significantly by city, county, and specific housing communities. Always check local ordinances to understand the rules where you live.
