*   **Reduce video quality:** Most streaming services allow you to lower the video resolution. This uses less bandwidth and can improve playback.

**Issue 2: Content Not Available in Your Region**
*   **Verify regional rights:** Streaming services have different content libraries based on location. The show you seek might not be licensed for your country.
