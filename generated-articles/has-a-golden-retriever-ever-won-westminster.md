## Understanding Westminster's Rigorous Best in Show Criteria

Winning Best in Show at Westminster is incredibly difficult. Judges follow very specific criteria. They look for dogs that perfectly match their breed's standard. This includes physical form, movement, and overall demeanor.

The competition starts with individual breed judging. Each dog is evaluated against its breed standard. The judge chooses the Best of Breed winner. These winners then move on to compete within their respective groups. There are seven groups: Sporting, Hound, Working, Terrier, Toy, Non-Sporting, and Herding. The Golden Retriever competes in the Sporting Group.

A judge selects one winner from each group. These seven group winners then meet in the final Best in Show ring. The Best in Show judge looks for a dog that is an outstanding example of its breed. They consider balance, health, temperament, and how the dog moves. The judge also assesses the dog's presence and overall condition. The dog must present itself perfectly.

The criteria are very precise. Every angle, every step, every hair matters. Dogs must be in peak physical condition. They must also have a calm and confident temperament. This allows them to show off their best qualities. It is a true test of a dog's breeding and training. Only the most exceptional dogs reach the Best in Show ring.

## FAQ Section

### Have any retrievers won Westminster Best in Show?

Yes, other retriever breeds have won Best in Show at Westminster. For example, a Flat-Coated Retriever won in 2011. A Chesapeake Bay Retriever also won the top prize in 1964. While the Golden Retriever has not won, other retriever cousins have achieved this esteemed title.

### What makes a dog win Westminster?

A dog wins Westminster by perfectly meeting its breed standard. Judges look for excellent conformation, graceful movement, and a confident temperament. The dog must be in peak physical condition. ### What is the highest a Golden Retriever has placed at Westminster?

### Which breeds often win Westminster?

Terrier breeds have historically won Best in Show most often at Westminster. Specific breeds like Fox Terriers (Wire and Smooth) have numerous wins. Poodles, Hounds, and some Working breeds also have a strong winning history. The winning breeds often possess distinct features or exceptional showmanship.
