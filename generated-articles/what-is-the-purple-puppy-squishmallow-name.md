## Caring for Your Purple Puppy: Keeping Kirk Cuddly

Proper care ensures your Kirk, the purple puppy Squishmallow, stays soft, vibrant, and cuddly for years. Squishmallows are made from delicate materials. They require gentle handling and cleaning. I always make sure to follow specific steps to maintain their quality. Regular maintenance keeps them looking their best.

Spot cleaning is always the first approach for minor dirt or spills. Use a damp cloth with a mild soap. Gently dab the affected area. Avoid rubbing harshly, as this can damage the plush fibers. Blot dry with a clean towel. Let Kirk air dry completely before snuggling him again. This method prevents colors from fading.

For a deeper clean, machine washing is possible but requires caution. Place Kirk inside a pillowcase or a mesh laundry bag. This protects his delicate fabric and stitching. Use cold water on a gentle cycle. Choose a mild, dye-free detergent. Harsh chemicals can affect the plush's softness.

Never use hot water, bleach, or fabric softener. Hot water can damage the fibers. Bleach can cause discoloration. Fabric softener can leave a residue. These products will diminish Kirk's squishiness. You want to preserve his original texture.

After washing, air drying is crucial. Do not put Kirk in a dryer. High heat can melt the synthetic fibers. This can permanently alter his shape and softness. Lay him flat on a clean, dry towel. Allow him to air dry fully. You can gently fluff him periodically. This helps maintain his iconic squishy feel.

Just like you would clean a [purple pillow](https://beacleaner.com/how-to-clean-purple-pillow) to keep its color and texture, the same care applies to Kirk. Regular, gentle cleaning keeps his purple vibrant. It prevents any buildup of dust or dirt. This ensures he remains a delightful, soft companion for a long time. Caring for your Squishmallow properly extends its life.
