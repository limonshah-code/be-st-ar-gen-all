### Unraveling the Genetics of Melanism in Geckos

The dark color of Black Night Leopard Geckos is a result of genetics. Specifically, it involves a recessive gene. A gecko must inherit two copies of this gene to show the Black Night trait. This genetic requirement makes breeding them challenging.

If a gecko has only one copy of the Black Night gene, it looks like a normal leopard gecko. These are called "het Black Night" geckos. They carry the gene but do not show the dark color. They are essential for breeding. [How old do leopard geckos have to be to breed](https://beacleaner.com/how-old-do-leopard-geckos-have-to-be-to-breed) is an important question for breeders.

When you breed two "het Black Night" geckos, the chances are specific. Only 25% of the offspring will be true Black Nights. 50% will be "het Black Night," and 25% will be normal geckos. This low percentage means many geckos must hatch to produce even one Black Night. This genetic lottery increases the value of each actual Black Night gecko. Breeders spend years pairing specific geckos. They track genetic lines carefully. Their goal is to get more of these dark, beautiful animals.

### The Intensive Process of Selective Breeding

Producing Black Night Leopard Geckos requires great effort. Breeders do not simply pair any two geckos. They engage in a meticulous process known as selective breeding. This means choosing specific parent geckos with desired traits.

Breeders must carefully identify "het Black Night" individuals. They use test breeding to confirm these genetics. This process takes much time and patience. It often spans several generations. Each pairing aims to improve the darkness and reduce patterns.

The breeders spend many years on these projects. They track the lineage of each gecko very closely. They document every hatchling. Geckos that do not meet the strict Black Night standard are sold as "hets" or normal geckos. This careful culling ensures the quality of the Black Night line.

This deep investment of time and resources drives up the cost. The breeder’s skill and dedication are reflected in the price. They are, in essence, selling years of hard work. This makes each Black Night gecko a product of significant effort.
