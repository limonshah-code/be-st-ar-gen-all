## The History and Prestige of Westminster

The Westminster Dog Show is more than just a competition. It is an American institution. Its history dates back to 1877. This makes it the second-longest continuously running sporting event in the United States, after the Kentucky Derby. The show began with a group of hunters who met regularly at the Westminster Hotel in New York City. They formed the Westminster Kennel Club.

Over the years, the show has grown significantly. It moved from its initial venue to Madison Square Garden. This iconic location became its home for many decades. The move highlighted its rising popularity. The show quickly became a prestigious event. Winning "Best in Show" is a dream for many dog owners and breeders. It brings immense recognition to the dog and its lineage. This high level of competition showcases the best of purebred dogs. Westminster celebrates purebred dogs and the sport of dog showing. It sets standards for many breeds. Judges look for physical conformation, temperament, and gait.
