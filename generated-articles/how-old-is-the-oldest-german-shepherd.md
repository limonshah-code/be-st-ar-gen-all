### Takeaway
To help your German Shepherd live a long and healthy life, remember these key points:
*   **Understand the Breed's Lifespan:** German Shepherds typically live 9 to 13 years, but some live much longer with proper care.
*   **Prioritize Preventive Care:** Regular vet visits, vaccinations, and parasite control are vital.
*   **Provide Optimal Nutrition:** A balanced diet specific to their age and health needs supports overall wellness.
*   **Ensure Appropriate Exercise:** Daily activity keeps their body strong and mind active without causing strain.
*   **Create a Supportive Environment:** Offer comfort, mental stimulation, and consistent emotional support as they age.
