## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are foot long hot dogs the same as jumbo hot dogs?
Yes, "foot long" and "jumbo" hot dogs often refer to the same larger size. They measure approximately 10 to 12 inches in length. Manufacturers use these terms interchangeably. Always check the package dimensions for exact measurements. This ensures you get the length you desire.

### Can I buy foot long hot dog buns easily?
Foot long hot dog buns are generally available where foot long hot dogs are sold. Most large grocery stores stock them. They are usually found in the bread aisle. Look for packages specifically labeled "foot long" or "jumbo." If not, specialty bakeries might offer them.

### What brands make foot long hot dogs?
Many popular hot dog brands offer foot long or jumbo versions. Brands like Nathan's Famous, Ball Park, Oscar Mayer, and Hebrew National are common. Local and regional meat companies also produce them. Check packaging for the "foot long" or "jumbo" designation.

### How do I store leftover foot long hot dogs?
Store leftover cooked foot long hot dogs in an airtight container. Refrigerate them promptly. They remain good for 3-4 days. Unopened packages can last longer in the fridge or freezer. Always follow package instructions for best storage practices.

### Are foot long hot dogs more expensive than regular hot dogs?
Yes, foot long hot dogs typically cost more per hot dog than standard sizes. This is due to their larger size and sometimes premium branding. However, buying in bulk from wholesale clubs can offer better value. Compare prices per pound for the best deals.

### Can I freeze foot long hot dogs?
Yes, you can freeze foot long hot dogs. Keep them in their original sealed packaging for best results. For opened packages, place them in an airtight freezer bag or container. They can last for several months in the freezer. Thaw them in the refrigerator before cooking.
