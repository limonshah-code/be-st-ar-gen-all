### Chepe Regional vs. Chepe Express

It is important to distinguish between the two Chepe services. The Chepe Regional is the older, slower service. It makes more stops and is used by locals. The Chepe Express is the newer, faster, luxury tourist service. It has fewer stops and better amenities.

The Chepe Express focuses on tourism. It typically offers a higher standard of service and security. Most safety discussions about "The Chepe" refer to the tourist-oriented Chepe Express.

### Classes of Service

The Chepe Express generally offers two main classes:
*   **First Class (Primera Clase Express):** This is the premium option. It includes comfortable seats, panoramic windows, and access to a dining car and bar. There is also a terrace car for open-air viewing. The service aims for a luxurious experience.
*   **Tourist Class (Clase Ejecutiva):** This class offers comfortable reclining seats. It has large windows for viewing. Passengers have access to a dining car. It provides a great balance of comfort and value.

Both classes maintain high safety standards. The difference lies in comfort and amenities.
