### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is it okay for my dog to sit on my chest?
Yes, it is generally okay for your dog to sit on your chest if you feel comfortable. This behavior often signals trust, affection, and a desire for security. As long as it does not cause you discomfort or restrict your breathing, it is a healthy sign of bonding. Ensure your dog is not too heavy or causing you pain.

#### Why does my dog only sit on my chest sometimes?
Your dog might sit on your chest only at certain times for various reasons. They might do it more when feeling anxious, seeking extra comfort during a storm, or when you are relaxed and attentive. It could also depend on their mood, your availability, or a specific need they have at that moment.

#### How can I encourage my dog to sit on my chest less often?
If you want to reduce this behavior, avoid reinforcing it with attention or treats. Gently redirect your dog to a nearby bed or seat. Reward them when they settle in the new spot. Consistency is key. You can also offer alternative forms of affection, like a belly rub next to you.

#### Does my dog sit on my chest because they are anxious?
Yes, anxiety can be a reason why your dog sits on your chest. When dogs feel scared or stressed, they seek comfort and security from their trusted human. Your closeness provides reassurance and helps them cope. Look for other signs of anxiety, like trembling or excessive panting.

#### What if my dog sits on my chest and then licks my face?
When your dog sits on your chest and then licks your face, it is a strong display of affection and submission. Licking is a common canine behavior for showing love, grooming, and acknowledging your leadership. It reinforces their bond with you and expresses their deep connection.
