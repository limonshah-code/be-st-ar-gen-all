## Understanding the Marriott-Sheraton Connection

The relationship between Marriott and Sheraton is quite straightforward today. Marriott International acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide in 2016. Sheraton was a flagship brand under the Starwood umbrella. This acquisition created the world's largest hotel company.

The integration meant all Starwood brands, including Sheraton, joined the Marriott loyalty program. This program is now known as Marriott Bonvoy. For guests, this was a significant change. It brought more choices under one loyalty program. This merger has simplified earning and redeeming points across many hotel types. You can read more about this relationship here: [Is Sheraton Owned by Marriott or Hilton?](https://beacleaner.com/is-sheraton-owned-by-marriott-or-hilton).

Before the merger, Sheraton had its own loyalty program, Starwood Preferred Guest (SPG). SPG was highly regarded by travelers. Marriott integrated SPG into its own program. This created the combined Marriott Bonvoy program. This transition aimed to offer a unified experience for all members.

Today, a stay at any Sheraton property is like a stay at a Marriott, Renaissance, or Courtyard. All these hotels fall under the same Bonvoy umbrella. This means your loyalty benefits extend across all participating brands. You can learn about the origins of Sheraton hotels and their evolution through time here: [What is the History of Sheraton Hotel?](https://beacleaner.com/what-is-the-history-of-sheraton-hotel). Understanding this background helps you appreciate the current loyalty setup.

## How to Earn Marriott Bonvoy Points at Sheraton Hotels

Earning Marriott Bonvoy points at Sheraton hotels is a simple process. It follows the same rules as earning points at any other Marriott brand. Your primary goal is to make sure your stay is eligible. Eligible stays are key to collecting points and elite night credits.

The most important step is to book your stay directly. This means using Marriott's official website, the Marriott Bonvoy app, or calling their reservations line. Booking through third-party websites like Expedia or Booking.com often makes your stay ineligible. Such bookings typically do not earn points or elite night credits. I always recommend booking direct to ensure you receive all your benefits.

### Base Earning Rates for Stays

You earn points based on the money you spend. Most Marriott Bonvoy brands, including Sheraton, award 10 points per US dollar spent on eligible charges. Eligible charges usually include your room rate and other services like dining, spa treatments, or business services. Always check your bill for clarity on what counts towards points.

For example, a $200 room night at a Sheraton will earn you 2,000 base points. These points add up quickly over several stays. Points are deposited into your account shortly after your check-out date. It is a good idea to monitor your account activity.

### Elite Status Bonuses

Your Marriott Bonvoy elite status gives you extra points. The higher your status, the more bonus points you receive.

*   **Silver Elite:** Earns a 10% bonus on base points.
*   **Gold Elite:** Earns a 25% bonus on base points.
*   **Platinum Elite:** Earns a 50% bonus on base points.
*   **Titanium Elite:** Earns a 75% bonus on base points.
*   **Ambassador Elite:** Earns a 75% bonus on base points.

If you are a Platinum Elite member, that $200 stay earns you 2,000 base points plus 1,000 bonus points (50% of 2,000). This brings your total to 3,000 points. Elite status makes a big difference in how fast you accumulate points. I find these bonuses incredibly valuable for my travel goals.

### Marriott Bonvoy Credit Cards

Using a Marriott Bonvoy co-branded credit card is another excellent way to earn points. These cards offer points for everyday spending. They also provide significant bonuses for Marriott purchases. Many cards offer 6 points per dollar spent at Marriott properties.

This means your $200 Sheraton stay could earn you 1,200 points just from the credit card. Combined with your base and elite bonus points, the total can be substantial. Some cards also offer annual free night certificates or automatic elite status. I personally use my Marriott credit card for all my hotel stays to maximize point accumulation.

## Redeeming Your Marriott Bonvoy Points at Sheraton and Beyond

Earning points is only half the fun; redeeming them is where the value truly shines. Your Marriott Bonvoy points open up a world of travel possibilities. You can use them for free nights at Sheraton hotels and across the entire Marriott portfolio. Understanding redemption values helps you make smart choices.

Marriott Bonvoy uses a dynamic pricing model for award nights. This means the number of points needed for a free night can vary. It depends on factors like demand, seasonality, and the specific hotel. A Sheraton hotel might cost 20,000 points one night and 30,000 points another. Always check the Marriott website or app for the most current point requirements.

### Free Night Awards

The most common way to redeem points is for free nights. To do this, simply search for your desired Sheraton property on the Marriott website. Then select "Use Points/Certificates" when entering your dates. The system will show you the point cost for each night. You can book a single night or multiple nights using points.

Marriott Bonvoy also offers "Cash + Points" options. This allows you to combine points and cash for a stay. This can be useful if you do not have enough points for a full free night. It also helps if you want to save some points for a future trip. I often use this option when I am a little short on points.

### Points for Upgrades and Other Benefits

Beyond free nights, your points can also fund other travel experiences. You can use points for room upgrades at some properties, though this is less common for standard Bonvoy points. You can also transfer your Marriott Bonvoy points to airline miles. Over 40 airline partners participate in this program. ## Common Pitfalls to Avoid When Earning Points

While earning Marriott Bonvoy points at Sheraton hotels is simple, some common mistakes can prevent you from getting your rewards. I have learned to avoid these pitfalls through experience. Knowing what to watch out for helps ensure every eligible stay counts. You want to make sure your efforts are rewarded.

The biggest mistake people make is booking through Online Travel Agencies (OTAs). These include popular sites like Expedia, Booking.com, or Hotels.com. When you book through these third-party channels, Marriott views your reservation as ineligible. This means you will not earn Marriott Bonvoy points. You also will not receive elite night credits. Even worse, hotels often do not recognize your elite status benefits for these bookings.

Always book directly through Marriott's official channels. Use the Marriott website, the Bonvoy app, or call their reservations line. This ensures your stay is eligible for points, elite credits, and benefits. It is a simple step that makes a huge difference.

### Forgetting to Add Your Bonvoy Number

Another common error is forgetting to add your Marriott Bonvoy member number to your reservation. If the hotel does not have your number, they cannot credit your account. You might still be able to add it at check-in. It is always best to include it when you book.

If you complete a stay and realize your points did not post, you can request a "retroactive credit." You typically have a window of time, often up to one year, to submit a missing stay request. You will need your reservation details and confirmation. While this usually works, it is an extra step you can easily avoid. I always double-check my reservation before confirming.

### Understanding Ineligible Rates

Not all rates qualify for earning points. Some deeply discounted rates, group rates, or wholesale rates may be ineligible. Always check the terms and conditions of your booking. The best way to ensure eligibility is to book a standard, publicly available rate.

Sometimes, you might book a corporate rate. Most corporate rates are eligible for points and elite credit. However, some special negotiated rates might not be. When in doubt, clarify with Marriott customer service before booking. Knowing these small details can save you from disappointment. You work hard for your points, so ensure you receive them.

## FAQ Section

### Q1: Can I use my Marriott Bonvoy elite status benefits at Sheraton?

Yes, absolutely. Sheraton hotels are part of the Marriott Bonvoy portfolio. Your Marriott Bonvoy elite status benefits apply at any Sheraton property worldwide. This includes perks like complimentary Wi-Fi, room upgrades, late checkout, and lounge access for higher tiers. Present your Bonvoy member number at check-in to ensure recognition.

### Q2: Do all Marriott Bonvoy brands earn points at the same rate?

Most Marriott Bonvoy brands, including Sheraton, earn 10 points per US dollar spent on eligible charges. However, some brands like Residence Inn, TownePlace Suites, and Element earn 5 points per US dollar. This is because these brands are extended-stay hotels. Always check the specific brand's earning rules for clarity.

### Q3: What if I forget to add my Bonvoy number to a Sheraton booking?

If you forgot to add your Bonvoy number, you can usually add it at check-in. If your stay is already complete, you can request a "missing stay" credit online. Log into your Marriott Bonvoy account and find the "Missing Stay Request" option. You will need your stay details to submit the claim.

### Q4: Can I earn points on dining or spa services without a stay?

Generally, you earn points on dining and spa services only when they are part of an eligible stay. These charges must be billed to your room. Some Marriott properties, however, have specific local programs. These programs might allow you to earn points on dining even without a stay. Check with the individual Sheraton hotel for details.

### Q5: How long do Marriott Bonvoy points last?

Marriott Bonvoy points generally expire after 24 consecutive months of inactivity. Inactivity means no earning or redeeming activity on your account. To keep your points active, simply complete an eligible stay, use a Marriott Bonvoy credit card, or redeem some points. Any qualifying activity extends your points' expiration date.
