### From Kennel Club to National Spotlight

The National Dog Show has roots in a much older competition. It began as the Kennel Club of Philadelphia Dog Show. This event has been held annually since 1876. This makes it one of the oldest dog shows in the United States. For many years, it was a regional event. Only dog enthusiasts knew about it.

In 2002, NBC began broadcasting the show nationally. This decision transformed it into the "National Dog Show Presented by Purina." The timing, right after the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, was crucial. This change put the show into millions of homes. It quickly became a beloved holiday tradition. It reached a much broader audience.

The national broadcast brought dog shows into mainstream popular culture. It educated viewers about different breeds. It also highlighted responsible pet ownership. The show's producers focused on family-friendly commentary. They made the judging process accessible to everyone. This shift made the show a household name. It is now a cherished part of Thanksgiving.

### Understanding the Judging Process

The judging process at the National Dog Show is meticulous. Judges evaluate dogs based on their breed standards. Each breed has a detailed description. This outlines the ideal appearance, temperament, and movement. Judges look for how well a dog conforms to these standards. They assess everything from coat texture to tail set.

The competition unfolds in several stages. First, dogs compete within their specific breed. The best dog of each breed wins "Best of Breed." These winners then advance to the Group competition. One dog from each of the seven groups wins "Best in Group." These seven winners then compete for the ultimate title. This final stage is "Best in Show."

Judges are highly experienced dog experts. They dedicate years to learning about breeds. Their decisions are based on deep knowledge and careful observation. It is not about personal preference. It is about how closely a dog matches its ideal standard. This systematic approach ensures fairness and professionalism in the competition. It reflects a true dedication to the breeds.

## FAQ Section

### When exactly does the National Dog Show start on Thanksgiving?
The National Dog Show, presented by Purina, typically starts at 12:00 PM Eastern Time. This is immediately after the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade concludes on NBC. Check your local listings for specific times, as regional variations apply, especially for different time zones.

### Which TV channel broadcasts the Thanksgiving Dog Show?
The National Dog Show is broadcast nationally on NBC. It has been a Thanksgiving Day tradition on this channel since 2002. You can also often stream the event live or on-demand through NBC's streaming service, Peacock, or other live TV streaming platforms.

### Can I stream the National Dog Show if I do not have cable TV?
Yes, you can stream the National Dog Show without cable TV. Live TV streaming services such as Peacock, Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, and Sling TV often carry NBC. Peacock typically offers the show for on-demand viewing shortly after the live broadcast as well.

### How long does the Thanksgiving Dog Show usually last?
The National Dog Show typically runs for two hours. It features various breed competitions and culminates in the Best in Show announcement. This two-hour duration makes it a perfect length for a holiday family viewing event.

### What is the difference between the National Dog Show and the Westminster Dog Show?
Both are prestigious dog shows. The National Dog Show is held on Thanksgiving and is known for its family-friendly broadcast on NBC. The Westminster Dog Show is held in February and is one of the oldest and most traditional dog shows, broadcast on Fox Sports. They have different organizations and traditions.
