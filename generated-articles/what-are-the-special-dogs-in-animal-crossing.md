### K.K. Slider: The Musical Canine Icon

K.K. Slider is perhaps the most famous of all the **special dogs in Animal Crossing**. He is a talented musician who performs live concerts. His songs are a cornerstone of the game's atmosphere. Every Saturday night, he visits your town or island.

Players gather to hear him play his guitar. K.K. Slider performs a wide range of musical genres. You can request specific songs or let him choose one for you. After each performance, he gives you a copy of the song. This lets you play it on your in-game music players. Collecting all of K.K. Slider's songs is a fun goal for many players. I always look forward to his Saturday night concerts. They bring such a lively vibe to the game.

His presence marks a significant milestone in Animal Crossing: New Horizons. To get K.K. Slider to visit your island, you must achieve a three-star island rating. This serves as a primary goal for many players early in the game. Once he performs, your island formally becomes a recognized destination. K.K. Slider's cool, laid-back personality makes him very popular. He truly embodies the relaxed spirit of Animal Crossing. His music helps set the perfect tone for your island adventures.

### Harvey: The Bohemian Photographer Dog

Harvey is a unique and **special dog in Animal Crossing** who owns Harv's Island. He is a free-spirited, bohemian character. He lives on an isolated island where he runs a special photo studio called Photopia. Here, players can set up elaborate photo shoots with their villagers. You can use any furniture you own. You can also invite other NPCs like K.K. Slider to join.

Harv's Island provides a creative outlet for players. It lets them stage fun scenes. You can capture memorable moments with your favorite characters. Harvey's calm and welcoming demeanor makes visiting his island a pleasant experience. He is distinct from the bustling main island life. He offers a serene escape. I enjoy experimenting with different setups at Photopia. It is a great way to express creativity.

In the Animal Crossing: New Horizons 2.0 update, Harv's Island expanded significantly. It now hosts a co-op of permanent shops. This area includes Leif's plant shop, Redd's art shop, and Kicks' accessory stand. Reaching these shops once required waiting for random visits. Now they are always available on Harv's Island. This expansion transformed Harv's Island into a useful hub. Harvey's vision created this community space. His role grew from just photography to supporting various vendors. He helps maintain a vibrant marketplace. His island embodies a spirit of collaboration and creativity.

### Digby: Happy Home Designer's Helpful Hound

Digby is another important **special dog in Animal Crossing**. He is Isabelle's twin brother. Digby greets players and helps them with client requests.

In Happy Home Designer, your job is to design homes for villagers. Digby guides you through the process. He helps you understand client preferences and house design specifics. He is very professional and organized, much like his sister, Isabelle. He wears a smart uniform, reflecting his serious work ethic. His role is central to the gameplay loop of Happy Home Designer. He makes the task of designing homes approachable and fun. I appreciated his clear instructions when playing Happy Home Designer. He ensured I understood each step.

### Resetti: The Reset Surveillance System's Gruff Canine

Mr. Resetti is a memorable character in Animal Crossing. While technically a mole, many players initially perceive him as a dog or badger due to his appearance. He is not a dog in the same way K.K. Slider or Isabelle are, but his impact is undeniable. Resetti works for the Reset Surveillance Center. His main job is to scold players who reset their game without saving.

Resetti appears suddenly and delivers long, angry lectures. He emphasizes the importance of saving your game. His outbursts were a distinctive feature of older Animal Crossing titles. These lectures were often quite intense. They served as a strong deterrent against resetting. Players learned quickly to save their progress. His over-the-top personality made him both feared and loved. He adds a layer of quirky humor to the game. I remember many times fearing his appearance after a careless reset.

In Animal Crossing: New Horizons, Resetti's role changed. The auto-save feature made his original job obsolete. Now, he operates the Rescue Service. If you get stuck on your island, he sends a helicopter to pick you up. He still has his gruff voice and direct manner. However, his new role is more helpful. He now provides a genuine service. This change reflects the evolving design of the Animal Crossing series. Even with a different job, Resetti remains one of the most recognizable "special" characters, often associated with canine-like traits by players. He represents the game's long-standing charm.

### FAQ Section

**Q1: How do I get K.K. Slider to visit my island in Animal Crossing: New Horizons?**
A1: To get K.K. Slider to visit, you must improve your island's rating to three stars. Isabelle provides feedback on your island's score. You need to decorate, plant flowers and trees, and add furniture outdoors. Attracting more villagers also helps. Once you reach three stars, K.K. Slider will schedule his first concert.

**Q2: What is Isabelle's main role in Animal Crossing: New Horizons?**
A2: Isabelle serves as your administrative assistant in Resident Services. She provides daily announcements and important news. She also helps with island evaluations. You can talk to her to change your island flag or tune. She also handles villager complaints about catchphrases or clothing.

**Q3: Where can I find Harvey in Animal Crossing?**
A3: Harvey lives on his own unique island, known as Harv's Island. This island is separate from your main island. You can access it by going to your island's airport and selecting "I wanna fly!" then choosing "Harv's Island." Once there, you can visit his Photopia studio or the co-op shopping area.

**Q4: Is Mr. Resetti a dog, a mole, or something else?**
A4: Mr. Resetti is officially a mole. His design, with his snout and gruff demeanor, sometimes leads players to mistake him for a dog or badger. He is part of the mole family in the Animal Crossing universe. His primary job involves giving lectures about saving your game.

**Q5: Are there other dog villagers besides the special characters?**
A5: Yes, Animal Crossing features many regular dog villagers who can live on your island. These include popular characters like Goldie, Lucky, Cherry, and Bea. They have unique personalities and house designs. These villagers move onto your island and contribute to its community like other animal types.

**Q6: What services does Harvey's Island offer after the update?**
A6: After the 2.0 update, Harv's Island expanded to include a permanent shopping area. This area hosts various vendors. You can find Leif selling shrubs and flowers, Redd selling art, Kicks offering shoes and accessories, and Sahara providing rugs and wallpapers. There is also a fortune-teller and a storage access service.
