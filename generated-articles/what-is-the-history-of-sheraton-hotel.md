### Sheraton Hotel: A Brief Overview

Sheraton Hotel began in 1937 when its founders purchased their first property in Springfield, Massachusetts. It quickly grew through strategic acquisitions and a focus on hospitality standards. The brand became a global leader, known for its consistent service and comfortable stays. Today, Sheraton is a key brand within Marriott International, continuing its legacy of connecting guests worldwide.

## Humble Beginnings: Founding the Sheraton Hotel Empire

The story of Sheraton Hotel begins with two visionaries, Ernest Henderson and Robert Moore. These men had a simple idea: offer comfortable accommodations and consistent service. In 1937, they bought their first hotel in Springfield, Massachusetts. This purchase marked the true start of what would become a global brand.

Their second hotel, also acquired that year, was notable. It had a distinct "Sheraton" sign on its roof. The partners decided to use this name for all their future properties. This choice gave birth to the Sheraton brand. Henderson and Moore quickly established a reputation for well-run hotels. They focused on providing quality lodging at a fair price. This foundation helped them expand rapidly. Their approach was simple yet effective: identify good properties and apply their hospitality model.

The early years saw a steady acquisition of hotels across the Northeast United States. Henderson and Moore understood the value of a strong brand identity even then. They ensured each new property met their growing standards. This early growth laid the groundwork for future success. The founders’ commitment to consistency became a hallmark of the Sheraton experience. They built a reliable name in the burgeoning hotel industry. This initial phase established Sheraton as a promising player in American hospitality.

## Rapid Expansion: Sheraton's Growth Through the Mid-20th Century

The post-World War II era brought significant growth for Sheraton Hotels. Returning soldiers and an expanding economy fueled a boom in travel. Sheraton was perfectly positioned to meet this new demand. The company aggressively acquired properties throughout the United States. It became the first hotel chain listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 1947. This was a major milestone for the brand.

Sheraton did not stop at domestic expansion. The company made history in 1949 by acquiring its first international hotels. It purchased properties in Toronto and Montreal, Canada. This move marked Sheraton as a pioneer in global hospitality. It demonstrated their ambition to reach beyond American borders. The brand continued to grow its portfolio steadily over the next two decades. Sheraton established a presence in major cities and popular tourist destinations. This period saw the standardization of many guest services.

The brand became synonymous with reliable lodging and convenient locations. By the 1960s, Sheraton was a dominant force in the hotel industry. Its properties offered travelers consistent comfort whether for business or leisure. The expansion was not just about adding more rooms. It was about building a cohesive brand experience across all locations. This strategy strengthened Sheraton's market position. The company showed how effective strategic growth could be in a competitive field.

## Challenges and Evolution: Sheraton Under ITT and Starwood

The late 1960s brought a new chapter for Sheraton. In 1968, International Telephone and Telegraph (ITT) Corporation acquired the brand. This acquisition brought significant changes to Sheraton's operations and identity. ITT, a large conglomerate, aimed to diversify its holdings. Sheraton became a part of a much larger corporate structure. This period saw both opportunities and challenges for the hotel chain.

Under ITT, Sheraton continued its global expansion. The brand established a presence in new markets across Europe, Asia, and Latin America. However, managing a hotel chain within a diverse conglomerate proved complex. Sheraton's brand identity sometimes struggled to maintain its unique focus. The hospitality market also grew more competitive during this time. New brands emerged, offering specialized services and experiences. Sheraton worked to adapt to these changing market dynamics.

A major turning point came in 1998 when Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide acquired ITT Sheraton. This acquisition revitalized the Sheraton brand. Starwood was a hospitality-focused company. They understood the nuances of hotel management and brand development. Starwood invested heavily in renovating Sheraton properties and refining its brand standards. This move helped Sheraton regain its prominence. The brand focused on improving guest experiences and modernizing its offerings. It was a crucial step in the brand's evolution, preparing it for the 21st century.

## Global Presence and Brand Reinvention: Sheraton in the Modern Era

As the 21st century began, Sheraton cemented its position as a truly global brand. With Starwood's support, the hotel chain undertook significant efforts to modernize its image. Sheraton focused on creating spaces that encouraged connection and productivity. This included updating lobbies and introducing new dining concepts. The goal was to meet the needs of contemporary travelers.

Sheraton hotels became known for their signature offerings. These included the "Link@Sheraton" communication hubs and the "Sheraton Club Lounge." These features provided guests with enhanced services and comfort. The brand also emphasized its commitment to community. It designed hotel spaces to serve as gathering places for both guests and locals. This reinvention aimed to differentiate Sheraton in a crowded market. It strengthened the brand's appeal to diverse segments of travelers.

The brand's presence continued to grow across all continents. From bustling city centers to serene resorts, Sheraton properties offered consistent quality. This global strategy ensured Sheraton remained a relevant and respected name in the hotel industry.

## Joining the Marriott Family: A New Chapter for Sheraton Hotels

A pivotal moment in the **history of Sheraton Hotel** occurred in 2016. Marriott International acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide. This monumental merger created the world's largest hotel company. Sheraton became a flagship brand within Marriott's extensive portfolio. This acquisition marked a new and exciting chapter for Sheraton. It brought the brand under the umbrella of a hospitality giant with vast resources.

The integration into Marriott's system offered Sheraton many benefits. It gained access to Marriott's powerful loyalty program, Marriott Bonvoy. This expanded its reach to millions of new customers. Marriott also committed to significant investments in the Sheraton brand. This included a multi-year plan to refresh and upgrade properties globally. The goal was to ensure Sheraton remained competitive and appealing. This strategic move aimed to elevate the guest experience further.

Being part of Marriott allowed Sheraton to leverage shared best practices and technologies. It also provided opportunities for broader market penetration. Marriott recognized Sheraton's enduring strength and global recognition. The company worked to preserve Sheraton's unique identity while enhancing its overall value. This acquisition has helped solidify Sheraton's future. It ensures the brand continues to be a leader in the global hospitality landscape. ## FAQ Section

### Who founded Sheraton Hotels, and when?
Sheraton Hotels was founded by Ernest Henderson and Robert Moore. They purchased their first hotel in Springfield, Massachusetts, in 1937. The "Sheraton" name came from a sign on one of their early acquisitions. Their vision was to create a consistent and reliable chain of hotels.

### When did Marriott International acquire Sheraton?
Marriott International acquired Sheraton Hotels in September 2016. This happened as part of Marriott's larger acquisition of Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide. This merger created the world's largest hotel company. Sheraton became a prominent brand within Marriott's extensive portfolio.

### What makes Sheraton different from other hotel brands?
Sheraton differentiates itself through its focus on community and connectivity. It provides inviting public spaces, like the Link@Sheraton, designed for guests to work, meet, and relax. The brand emphasizes a consistent and comfortable guest experience globally. It often features amenities such as the Sheraton Club Lounge.

### How many Sheraton hotels are there worldwide?
As of recent reports (post-Marriott acquisition), Sheraton operates over 400 hotels in more than 70 countries and territories. It continues to be one of Marriott International's largest and most geographically diverse brands. The number fluctuates slightly with new openings and renovations.

### What recent changes has Sheraton undergone?
Sheraton has undergone a significant brand refresh under Marriott's ownership. This includes extensive renovations to update property designs and enhance guest rooms. The brand focuses on creating modern, inviting public spaces. It aims to offer more flexible work and social environments for today's travelers.

### Is Sheraton considered a luxury brand?
Sheraton is generally considered an upscale, full-service hotel brand, not a luxury brand. While it offers premium amenities and services, true luxury brands typically offer more exclusive experiences. Marriott International's portfolio includes distinct luxury brands such as St. Regis and The Ritz-Carlton, positioning Sheraton in the premium, global segment.
