### What breed is Chloe from Beverly Hills Chihuahua?
Chloe is a purebred Chihuahua, specifically depicted as a "deer head" Chihuahua. This type of Chihuahua has a longer snout and larger ears, resembling a young deer. This differs slightly from the "apple head" Chihuahua, which has a rounder head and a shorter snout. Chloe’s breed is central to her pampered background and appearance.
