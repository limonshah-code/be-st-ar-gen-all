### Understanding Brand Ownership: The Marriott-Sheraton Connection

Many travelers ask about the relationship between Marriott and Sheraton. It is a common source of confusion. The key fact is that Marriott International is a massive hotel company. This company owns many different hotel brands. Sheraton is one of these brands.

Marriott International acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide in 2016. Sheraton was a flagship brand of Starwood. This acquisition brought Sheraton under the Marriott umbrella. Now, Sheraton operates as part of the Marriott International family. This means Marriott does not directly compete with Sheraton in the same way independent companies might. Instead, Sheraton fills a specific niche within Marriott's diverse offerings. I find this acquisition reshaped the entire hospitality industry.

#### Sheraton's Journey into Marriott Bonvoy

Sheraton began its journey as an independent hotel chain. It grew over many decades. Its long history established it as a well-known name globally. The brand became synonymous with full-service hotels for business and leisure. You can learn more about [Sheraton's long history](https://beacleaner.com/what-is-the-history-of-sheraton-hotel).

When Marriott acquired Starwood, all Starwood brands, including Sheraton, joined the Marriott loyalty ecosystem. This integration was a major event for guests. It unified loyalty programs and brought new opportunities for travelers. This move consolidated many hotel options under one powerful system.

For travelers, this change brought significant benefits. It created a single, comprehensive loyalty program, Marriott Bonvoy. Guests can now earn and redeem points at Sheraton properties. This is the same as earning points at any other Marriott brand. It simplifies travel for frequent guests. The acquisition also led to brand standardization and integration. This ensures a consistent experience across the global portfolio.

### Marriott Bonvoy vs. Sheraton Loyalty: A Unified Program

The question of loyalty programs between Marriott and Sheraton is simple. There is no separate "Sheraton Loyalty" program anymore. Both brands operate under the umbrella of Marriott Bonvoy. Marriott Bonvoy is Marriott International's global loyalty program. It covers over 30 distinct hotel brands worldwide.

When you stay at a Sheraton hotel, you earn Marriott Bonvoy points. You also earn elite night credits toward Bonvoy elite status. This is exactly what happens when you stay at any other Marriott brand, such as Courtyard, Ritz-Carlton, or JW Marriott. Your elite status benefits also apply at Sheraton properties. This creates a seamless experience for loyal guests.

#### Benefits of a Unified Loyalty Program

The unified Marriott Bonvoy program offers many advantages. Members can enjoy a wide range of benefits across different brands. For example, I have used my Marriott Bonvoy points for stays at both Sheraton and luxury Marriott properties. This flexibility is a key feature.

*   **Point Earning and Redemption:** You earn points on eligible stays at Sheraton, like any other Marriott brand. You can redeem these points for free nights, experiences, or travel packages.
*   **Elite Status Recognition:** Your Marriott Bonvoy elite status, from Silver to Titanium or Ambassador, is recognized at all participating Sheraton hotels. This includes benefits like late checkout, room upgrades, and complimentary breakfast, depending on your status tier.
*   **Broader Hotel Choice:** A single program means you have more options for earning and redeeming points. You are not limited to one brand. This flexibility lets you choose hotels based on location, price, or specific amenities, all while building your loyalty rewards.

The integration of Sheraton into Marriott Bonvoy simplifies the loyalty landscape for travelers. It means you can focus on enjoying your stay. You do not need to manage multiple loyalty accounts. This unified approach strengthens Marriott's position in the hospitality market.

Each brand aims to provide a tailored experience. This means that a guest looking for a trendy, urban vibe might choose an AC Hotel. A guest seeking ultimate luxury might prefer a St. Regis.

### FAQ Section

#### Is Sheraton considered a luxury brand?
Sheraton is generally not considered a luxury brand. It sits within Marriott International's "Premium" brand segment. This segment offers full-service hotels with comprehensive amenities. It provides a comfortable and reliable experience. Luxury brands within Marriott's portfolio include The Ritz-Carlton and St. Regis.

#### Can I earn Marriott Bonvoy points at Sheraton?
Yes, absolutely. Sheraton is part of Marriott International's brand portfolio. This means all Sheraton hotels participate in the Marriott Bonvoy loyalty program. You can earn and redeem Marriott Bonvoy points. You also receive elite status benefits at Sheraton properties.

#### Are all Sheraton hotels the same?
No, not all Sheraton hotels are exactly the same. While they adhere to brand standards, individual properties vary in age, size, and specific amenities. Many Sheratons are undergoing modernization efforts. These efforts update their design and offerings. Newer or renovated properties will provide a more contemporary experience.

#### Which brand offers better amenities, Marriott or Sheraton?
This question is tricky because "Marriott" refers to many brands. A luxury Marriott brand like JW Marriott offers more amenities than a Sheraton. A select-service Marriott brand like Courtyard offers fewer. Sheraton offers a strong set of full-service amenities. These include club lounges, restaurants, and fitness centers.

#### Is Marriott more expensive than Sheraton?
Marriott International offers a wide range of hotel brands. Some Marriott brands are more expensive than Sheraton (e.g., St. Regis). Some are less expensive (e.g., Fairfield Inn). Sheraton typically sits in the mid-to-upper price range within the overall Marriott portfolio.

#### Did Marriott buy Sheraton?
Yes, Marriott International acquired Sheraton's parent company, Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide, in 2016. This acquisition brought Sheraton and other Starwood brands under the Marriott International umbrella. Sheraton is now a key brand within the Marriott family.
