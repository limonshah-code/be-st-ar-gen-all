## FAQs About ASCA and Australian Shepherds

### What is the primary focus of ASCA?
ASCA's primary focus is the preservation and advancement of the Australian Shepherd breed. This includes maintaining the breed's working characteristics, health, and temperament. They promote responsible breeding practices and offer diverse dog sports. These efforts ensure the breed remains true to its heritage.

### Does ASCA only register Australian Shepherds?
Yes, ASCA specifically registers Australian Shepherds. It is a single-breed club. This specialized focus allows them to dedicate all their resources to this one breed. They have comprehensive standards and programs tailored to Australian Shepherds. Other organizations handle different breeds.

### Can a dog be registered with both ASCA and AKC?
Yes, a dog can be dual-registered with both ASCA and AKC. Many Australian Shepherd owners choose to do this. Dual registration allows dogs to compete in events sanctioned by either organization. The requirements and processes for each club are separate.

### What types of events does ASCA offer for dogs?
ASCA offers a wide range of events. These include herding trials, conformation shows, obedience, agility, and rally. They also have junior handler programs. These events test different aspects of the Australian Shepherd's abilities. They provide a fun and competitive outlet for dogs and owners.

### Is ASCA membership required to attend ASCA events?
While you do not always need membership to *attend* as a spectator, you must be an ASCA member and your dog must be ASCA-registered to *compete* in most sanctioned events. Membership helps support the organization. It also grants you access to club resources and voting rights.

### How does ASCA contribute to Australian Shepherd health?
ASCA promotes breed health through several initiatives. They encourage genetic testing for common breed diseases. They educate breeders on health clearances and responsible breeding practices. Their focus on sound structure in conformation also supports the physical health of the breed.
