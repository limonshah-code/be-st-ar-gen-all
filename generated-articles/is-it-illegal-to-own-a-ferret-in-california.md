### Impact on Native Wildlife

### The California Fish and Game Code

Section 2118 of the California Fish and Game Code specifically lists animals that cannot be imported, transported, or possessed. These are animals deemed "injurious" to the state's native wildlife, agriculture, or public health and safety. Ferrets (Mustela putorius furo) are explicitly included in this list. This legal classification makes them illegal as pets.

The law aims to prevent the introduction of species that could become invasive. It also protects against diseases that could harm humans or livestock. This is a preventative measure. The state tries to avoid problems before they start. My own research confirms this protective intent.

The code outlines specific regulations. It states that no person shall import, transport, or possess any wild animal of the family Mustelidae. This family includes ferrets. The law is very specific about which animals are banned. This clarity leaves little room for interpretation.
