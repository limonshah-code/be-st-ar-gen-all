The Mahlemut people lived in the western coastal region of Alaska. They relied on dogs for survival. These dogs pulled sleds loaded with supplies. They also helped hunt seals and other game. The Malamute breed developed from these working dogs. They were essential to the Mahlemuts' way of life.
