## Understanding Crested Gecko Sleep: Do Their Eyes Stay Open?

Have you ever watched your crested gecko and wondered if it truly sleeps? Unlike many pets, these fascinating reptiles have a unique way of resting. It often leaves new owners puzzled about their sleep habits. I recall observing my own gecko, wondering if those unblinking eyes meant it was always awake. This common question prompts closer inspection of their biology.

The question of whether crested geckos sleep with their eyes open is common. Many reptile species exhibit this trait due to specialized eye structures. This article will explore the specifics of crested gecko eyes and how they manage to "sleep" without closing them. We will look at their unique anatomy, typical sleep patterns, and what healthy rest looks like for your pet. By the end, you will have a clear understanding of your gecko's resting behavior.

### Takeaway:

*   Crested geckos do sleep with their eyes open.
*   They lack eyelids and instead have a clear, protective scale called a "brille."
*   Healthy crested geckos typically rest during the day in hidden, dark spots.
*   Observe their body language and reduced activity to recognize sleep.
*   Proper husbandry supports good sleep and overall health.

### Yes, Crested Geckos Sleep With Their Eyes Open.

Crested geckos, like many other gecko species, do sleep with their eyes open. They do not possess movable eyelids. Instead, a transparent, fused scale covers and protects each eye. This special scale, known as a "brille" or spectacle, keeps their eyes moist and shielded from debris even when resting.
