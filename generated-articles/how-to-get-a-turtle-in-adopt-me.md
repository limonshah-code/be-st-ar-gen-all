No, the Adopt Me Turtle is no longer available from eggs. It was exclusively found in the Aussie Egg, which retired from the game in March 2021. This means you cannot hatch a new Turtle from any current in-game eggs.

Good pets to trade for an Adopt Me Turtle include other legendary pets, especially out-of-game ones. High-tier pets from current eggs with added Ride or Fly potions are also strong offers. You can also offer multiple rare pets. Always check current Adopt Me trade value lists.

### Q3: How much Robux does a Turtle cost?
An Adopt Me Turtle does not have a direct Robux price anymore. It was part of the 750 Robux Aussie Egg, but that egg is retired. You can only get a Turtle through player trading. Its value in trades is determined by current player demand, not a fixed Robux cost.

### Q5: Can I get a free Adopt Me Turtle?
No, it is extremely unlikely to get a free Adopt Me Turtle. Since Turtles are legendary and out-of-game, they hold high value. Players will not give them away for free. You must trade valuable pets or items to acquire one. Be wary of anyone offering free pets, as it might be a scam.

Creating a Neon Turtle requires four fully-grown Turtles. Each Turtle takes a significant amount of time to age from newborn to full-grown. This process involves completing many tasks.
