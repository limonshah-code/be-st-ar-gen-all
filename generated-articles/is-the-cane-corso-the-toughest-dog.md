### Defining What Makes a Dog "Tough"

Many people think of "toughness" as sheer physical strength. This is only one part of the story for dogs. A truly tough dog shows many qualities. These include physical strength, mental resilience, and protective instincts.

Physical toughness means a dog has a strong body. It can endure harsh conditions or strenuous activities. A tough dog can handle pain and bounce back quickly from challenges. This includes robust health and a powerful build.

Mental toughness is just as important. It means a dog has a stable temperament. It stays calm under pressure. A mentally tough dog can think clearly and follow commands even in chaotic situations. It shows courage and does not give up easily. This type of dog handles stress well.

Protective instincts also define toughness. A tough dog will stand its ground to protect its family or territory. It shows bravery when faced with threats. This protective nature comes from its inherent drive to guard. It means the dog is ready to act when danger arises. When we consider the Cane Corso, we must examine all these traits. We seek to understand if it embodies true toughness.
