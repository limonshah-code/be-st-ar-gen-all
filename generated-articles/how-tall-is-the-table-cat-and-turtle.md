## FAQ Section

### Q: Do all cats jump on tables?
A: Most cats are naturally agile and enjoy elevated spots. Many cats will jump on tables and counters if given the opportunity. This provides them with a better view of their surroundings. Some cats may be less inclined due to age or health. Training can also influence this behavior.

### Q: How high can a cat jump?
A: An average healthy cat can jump about five to six times its own height in a single bound. This means a cat that is 10 inches tall could easily jump 50-60 inches high. Their powerful leg muscles allow them to achieve impressive vertical leaps. This helps them access high places.

### Q: Are turtles safe on tables?
A: Turtles are not safe on tables without constant supervision. They cannot perceive edges well. A fall from a table can cause severe shell damage or other injuries. If you place a turtle on a table, ensure the surface is flat and secure. Also, create a barrier around the edges to prevent accidental falls.

### Q: What is the average height of a domestic cat?
A: The average domestic cat typically stands between 9 to 16 inches (23-41 cm) tall at the shoulder. This range includes various mixed breeds and common purebreds. Factors like genetics, diet, and overall health contribute to their final adult height. Males are generally larger than females.

### Q: How do you measure a turtle's height?
A: To measure a turtle's height, place it on a flat surface. Then measure from the surface to the highest point of its shell. This is usually the center of the dome. Shell height varies greatly by species. It also depends on the turtle's age and overall development.

### Q: Should I let my pets on tables?
A: The decision to allow pets on tables depends on the pet and your preferences. For cats, ensure the table is stable and clear of hazards. For turtles, extreme caution and supervision are vital due to fall risks. Always prioritize your pet's safety and your home's hygiene.
