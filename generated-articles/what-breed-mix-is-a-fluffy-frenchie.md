### Takeaway: Understanding the Fluffy Frenchie's True Origin

*   Fluffy Frenchies are **not a mixed breed**. They are purebred French Bulldogs.
*   Their long coat comes from a naturally occurring, **recessive gene (FGF5)**.
*   Both parents must carry this specific gene for puppies to be fluffy.
*   They share the same health and temperament traits as standard French Bulldogs.
*   Finding a reputable breeder is essential for ethical ownership.
