## Master Snapping Turtle Fishing in Animal Crossing

Are you trying to complete your Critterpedia in Animal Crossing New Horizons? Do you want to fill your museum with every possible creature? The snapping turtle is a sought-after catch for many players. This unique river dweller adds a touch of excitement to your island's ecosystem.

Catching a snapping turtle in Animal Crossing can feel tricky at first. It requires knowing specific conditions. This guide will help you understand where and when to find this elusive creature. We will cover locations, seasons, times, and effective fishing methods. By following these steps, you will soon add a snapping turtle to your collection.

### Takeaway: Your Quick Guide to Catching the Snapping Turtle

*   Target river locations on your island, including river mouths.
*   Fish during the evening and night hours, specifically from 9 PM to 4 AM.
*   Focus your efforts on the spring, summer, and fall months.
*   Look for a distinct large, size 4 shadow in the water.
*   Use fish bait to increase the frequency of fish spawns.

### How to Catch a Snapping Turtle in Animal Crossing

To catch a snapping turtle in Animal Crossing, fish in rivers and river mouths during the evening (9 PM) to early morning (4 AM) from April to October in the Northern Hemisphere, or October to April in the Southern Hemisphere. Look for a large, size 4 shadow to increase your chances.
