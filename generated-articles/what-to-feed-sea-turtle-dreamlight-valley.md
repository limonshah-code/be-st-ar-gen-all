## Approaching and Interacting with Sea Turtles

Sea Turtles have a unique way of interacting with you. Unlike some other critters, they do not run away immediately. Instead, they retreat into their shells. This behavior is a key part of approaching them successfully. I learned this trick early on.

When you first walk up to a Sea Turtle, it will pull into its shell. You will see its head disappear. Do not move during this time. Just stand still and wait. The waiting period is usually very short. It might feel a bit silly, but it works every time.

After a few seconds, the Sea Turtle will slowly emerge from its shell. Its head will pop back out. This is your cue to offer them food. Walk up to them and press the interaction button. A menu will appear, letting you choose an item to feed them. Always select their favorite food for the best results.

*   **Be Patient:** Patience is the most important virtue when interacting with Sea Turtles. Rushing will only make them hide longer. I always take a deep breath and wait.
*   **Stand Still:** Do not move once they retract. Any movement might reset their behavior. They need to feel safe before they come out.
*   **Offer Favorite Food:** Once they emerge, select Seaweed or Herring. Their favorite gives the highest friendship points.

Understanding their behavior makes the interaction smooth. It prevents frustration and ensures a successful feeding. This method works for all Sea Turtle variants. It is a consistent way to build rapport.
