### Air Travel and ESAs: Understanding Recent Changes

The rules for ESAs on airplanes have changed significantly. Previously, the Air Carrier Access Act (ACAA) protected ESAs on flights. This meant airlines had to allow ESAs to fly in the cabin with their owners. However, these rules were updated in 2021. The Department of Transportation (DOT) now states that airlines are not required to accommodate ESAs.

Airlines now treat ESAs as regular pets. This means your dog will likely need to fly in a carrier, if small enough. Larger dogs may need to travel in cargo. You will also be subject to pet fees. These fees are the same as for any other pet. My friend was surprised by these changes. She had to pay a fee for her ESA on a recent flight.

Service animals, however, are still protected. They can fly in the cabin free of charge. Airlines can still choose to allow ESAs, but it is at their discretion. Always check with your specific airline before booking a flight. Understand their current pet policies. This will help you avoid unexpected issues at the airport. These changes are crucial to know if you travel frequently.
