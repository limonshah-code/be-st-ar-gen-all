Understanding the laws is crucial. Different laws govern service animals and emotional support animals. The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) protects service dogs. The **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** applies to public places. It protects individuals with disabilities. It guarantees their right to use a service dog.
