### What Was the Bell Beefer and Why Is It Gone?
The Bell Beefer was a unique Taco Bell item. It was essentially a loose meat sandwich. It featured seasoned ground beef, diced onions, and red sauce. These were served on a hamburger bun. It was Taco Bell's attempt to compete with traditional burger joints. It was popular in the 1970s and 80s. Taco Bell removed the Bell Beefer in the mid-1990s. This removal allowed the brand to focus on its Mexican-inspired core menu items.

### Did Taco Bell Ever Sell Salsa Roja or Verde Sauce?
Yes, Taco Bell used to offer both Salsa Roja and Verde Sauce. These sauces were available at the condiment bar. Customers could add them to their food. Salsa Roja was a classic red sauce. Verde Sauce offered a milder, tangy green flavor. Taco Bell removed both sauces from most locations around 2016-2017. This was part of a menu and condiment simplification effort. Many customers miss having these options available.

### Why Was the Mexican Pizza Discontinued (and then brought back)?
Taco Bell discontinued the Mexican Pizza in 2020. This was part of a menu simplification. The item involved specific ingredients and preparation steps. These steps added complexity to kitchen operations. Its removal caused a huge outcry from fans. Due to massive customer demand and a social media campaign, Taco Bell brought the Mexican Pizza back in 2022. Its return was a major event for the brand.
