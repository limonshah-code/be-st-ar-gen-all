### The Maker of Taste of the Wild

Taste of the Wild dog food is manufactured by **Diamond Pet Foods**. This company is a family-owned and privately-held business based in Meta, Missouri. Diamond Pet Foods produces several other popular pet food brands in addition to Taste of the Wild.

## Diamond Pet Foods: The Manufacturer Behind the Brand

When we talk about who makes Taste of the Wild dog food, the answer points directly to **Diamond Pet Foods**. This company is a significant player in the pet food industry. It is known for producing a variety of popular brands, including Taste of the Wild, Diamond Naturals, and Kirkland Signature pet foods. Diamond Pet Foods has been operating for decades, building a reputation for large-scale production.

The company's story began in 1970. Brothers-in-law Gary Schell and Richard Kampeter founded it in Meta, Missouri. They started with a vision to provide high-quality pet food at an affordable price. Over the years, Diamond Pet Foods has expanded its operations. It now owns several manufacturing facilities across the United States. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Taste of the Wild an independently owned company?

Taste of the Wild is a brand owned by Diamond Pet Foods. Diamond Pet Foods is a privately-held, family-owned company. It is not publicly traded on the stock market. This means its operations and decisions are generally made independently, rather than being driven by external shareholders.

### Are Taste of the Wild recipes truly grain-free?

Yes, all Taste of the Wild dog food recipes are formulated to be grain-free. The brand explicitly states this as a core part of its ancestral diet philosophy. Instead of grains, they use alternative carbohydrate sources like sweet potatoes, peas, and lentils. This caters to dogs with grain sensitivities or owners who prefer this type of diet.

### Has Taste of the Wild had any recalls?

Yes, like many large pet food manufacturers, Diamond Pet Foods (which makes Taste of the Wild) has experienced product recalls in the past. The most notable recall for Taste of the Wild occurred in 2012 due to potential salmonella contamination. The company took steps to address the issues and enhance its safety protocols following these events.

### Where does Taste of the Wild source its ingredients?

Taste of the Wild states it sources high-quality ingredients from various reliable suppliers. These suppliers are primarily located within the United States. They obtain proteins like bison, venison, and salmon, along with fruits and vegetables such as sweet potatoes and peas. The company maintains strict standards for ingredient quality and safety from its sources.

### How do I choose the right Taste of the Wild formula for my dog?

Choosing the right formula depends on your dog's age, activity level, and specific dietary needs. Consider puppy formulas for young dogs and senior options for older pets. Look at the primary protein source to see what your dog prefers. If your dog has sensitivities, pay attention to the exact ingredients. Consulting your veterinarian is always the best approach for personalized recommendations.

### Is Taste of the Wild good for puppies?

Taste of the Wild offers specific formulas designed for puppies, such as the Pacific Stream Puppy Formula with Smoked Salmon. Always ensure the formula you choose is explicitly labeled for puppies to meet their unique nutritional requirements.
