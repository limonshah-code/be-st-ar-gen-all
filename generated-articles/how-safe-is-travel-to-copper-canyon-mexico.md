### Understanding the Copper Canyon Region

Copper Canyon, or Barrancas del Cobre, is a collection of six distinct canyons. It is four times larger and deeper than the Grand Canyon in Arizona. This area is home to the indigenous Rarámuri people. They maintain a traditional way of life.

The region's unique geography makes it a prime destination for adventure tourism. You can hike, bike, and ride horses. The famous Chepe train offers a comfortable way to see the views. Understanding this vastness helps you plan your specific travel route.
