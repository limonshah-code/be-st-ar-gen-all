Without these dogs, the Mahlemut people would not have been able to navigate their harsh environment. This direct link to an indigenous group makes the name powerful.

## Living with an Alaskan Malamute: Care and Considerations

First, Alaskan Malamutes need a lot of exercise. They are working dogs at heart. They have boundless energy. A simple walk around the block is not enough. They need vigorous activity daily.
