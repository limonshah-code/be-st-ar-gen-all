Yes, you can give Dogmeat various pieces of armor in Fallout 4. These include dog helmets, goggles, and body armor. Equipping these items increases his damage resistance. This makes him more durable in combat. You can find dog armor throughout the wasteland or craft some pieces.

### What happens if I dismiss Dogmeat?
If you dismiss Dogmeat, you can send him to any of your owned settlements. He will travel there and wait for you. You can pick him up again at any time from that settlement. Just find him and initiate dialogue to recruit him back into your party. He will always be available to join you again.
