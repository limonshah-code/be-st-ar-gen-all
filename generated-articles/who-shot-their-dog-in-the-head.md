### Recognizing Signs of Animal Abuse

It is important to recognize when an animal suffers abuse. The signs are often visible. They can also be behavioral.

*   **Physical Indicators:**
    *   Unexplained limping or inability to stand.
    *   Wounds, bruises, or broken bones.
    *   Visible parasites, like fleas or severe ticks.
    *   Extreme thinness or starvation.
    *   Matted fur or skin infections.
    *   Signs of an untreated illness.
*   **Environmental Indicators:**
    *   Lack of clean water or food.
    *   No shelter from extreme weather.
    *   Unsanitary living conditions, like excessive feces.
    *   Animals kept in cages that are too small.
*   **Behavioral Indicators:**
    *   Extreme fear or aggression.
    *   Cowering or flinching when approached.
    *   Lethargy or depression.
    *   Over-grooming due to stress.

When you notice these signs, action is necessary. Every animal deserves a safe and healthy life. My hope is that people will not ignore these signals. We must speak for those who cannot speak for themselves. This collective effort saves lives. We can stop animals from living in fear and pain.
