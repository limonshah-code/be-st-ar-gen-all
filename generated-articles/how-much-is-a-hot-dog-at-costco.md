### Why the Price Never Changes: A Costco Promise

The steadfast $1.50 price tag for the hot dog and soda combo is a strategic business decision. It is more than just a menu item; it is a symbol of Costco's commitment to its members. Former CEO Jim Sinegal famously insisted on keeping the price fixed. He once told the current CEO, W. Craig Jelinek, "If you raise the hot dog, I will kill you." This anecdote highlights the deep-seated company philosophy.

Costco views the hot dog as a "loss leader." This means they do not make much, if any, profit from it. The goal is not to earn money directly from the hot dog. Instead, it serves to attract customers into the warehouse. People come for the hot dog, and then they often stay to shop. This strategy works very well.

The low price also builds customer loyalty. Members feel they get incredible value every time they visit. This positive feeling extends to their perception of the entire Costco brand. I know many people who will grab a hot dog before or after their shopping trip. It has become a ritual for them. The hot dog acts as an affordable, quick reward for their visit. This unwavering price point helps Costco maintain its image as a place for great deals. It assures members that they always get the best value possible.

### FAQ Section

#### Q1: Do I need a Costco membership to buy a hot dog?
No, in many Costco locations, you do not need a membership card to purchase items from the food court. Access to the food court is often outside the main warehouse. Some older or specific locations might still require membership. It is always a good idea to check with your local store.

#### Q2: What kind of hot dog does Costco use?
Costco uses a quarter-pound, all-beef hot dog. This hot dog is made specifically for Costco and is known for its size and flavor. It is served on a fresh bun, offering a substantial and satisfying meal that many customers enjoy.

#### Q3: Can I get just a hot dog without the soda?
Yes, you can often purchase just the hot dog without the soda if you prefer. However, the price typically remains the same at $1.50 for the hot dog and soda combo. The combo is such a great deal that most people opt for it, even if they only drink a little of the soda.

#### Q4: Why is the Costco hot dog so cheap?
The Costco hot dog is so cheap because it serves as a "loss leader" for the company. Costco intentionally sells it at a very low profit, or even at a loss. The purpose is to attract customers into the warehouse, knowing they will likely make other purchases. It builds customer loyalty.

#### Q5: What happened to the Polish hot dog at Costco?
Costco discontinued the Polish hot dog from its food court menu in 2018. The company decided to simplify its menu to focus on its most popular items and to offer healthier options. This change caused some disappointment among long-time fans of the Polish dog.

#### Q6: Are there vegetarian options at the Costco food court?
Yes, Costco has introduced some vegetarian options to its food court menu. These can vary by location. Common vegetarian items include the cheese pizza slice. Some locations might offer a vegetarian salad. Always check the current menu at your specific Costco food court for available choices.
