## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it good when my dog lays on my feet?

Yes, it is generally a good sign. When your dog lays on your feet, they show trust, affection, and comfort. It means they feel safe and loved in your presence. This behavior reinforces your bond. It indicates a strong, positive relationship.

### Does my dog lay on my feet because they love me?

Absolutely! This is one of the clearest signs of affection. Dogs express love through physical closeness. They want to be near the people they cherish. Laying on your feet is a heartfelt gesture of their devotion. It shows they truly love you.

### What does it mean if my dog sleeps only on my feet?

If your dog only sleeps on your feet, it indicates an extra strong bond and deep trust. They might feel exceptionally secure with you. It could also mean they find your specific warmth or scent particularly comforting. This behavior often points to a very close and dependent relationship.

### Why do dogs suddenly start laying on your feet?

A sudden change could be due to several reasons. They might be feeling anxious or insecure, seeking extra comfort. They could also be showing increased affection or trying to get more attention. Observe other behaviors to understand the specific cause. It is usually a plea for closeness or reassurance.

### Should I encourage my dog to lay on my feet?

Encouraging this behavior is fine if you enjoy it. It is a natural and bonding action for dogs. If you find it disruptive or uncomfortable, you can gently redirect them to a nearby dog bed. Always use positive reinforcement. This maintains a positive relationship with your dog.

### What if my dog sometimes lays on my feet and sometimes doesn't?

This is normal behavior. Dogs have varying needs for closeness and comfort based on their mood or environment. They might seek your feet more when feeling tired, cold, or anxious. Other times, they might prefer their own space. This fluctuation simply shows their diverse needs.
