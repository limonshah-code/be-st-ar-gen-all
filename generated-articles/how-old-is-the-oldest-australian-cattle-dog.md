### Caring for a Senior Australian Cattle Dog: Extending Healthy Years

As your Australian Cattle Dog ages, their needs change. Providing specific care for a senior **ACD** can significantly improve their comfort and extend their healthy years. #### Adjusting Diet for Senior ACDs

Older dogs often have slower metabolisms. Their diet should reflect this change. Many veterinarians recommend senior dog food formulas. These foods often have fewer calories but remain rich in essential nutrients. Supplements like glucosamine and chondroitin can support joint health. These supplements become especially important as dogs experience more wear and tear.

#### Gentle Exercise and Mental Engagement

While senior ACDs might not have the boundless energy of their youth, exercise remains crucial. Opt for shorter, more frequent walks. Gentle swimming or soft play can keep them active without overstraining joints. Mental stimulation is also important. Puzzle toys and slow-paced training sessions keep their minds engaged. This helps maintain cognitive function.

#### Regular Vet Check-ups for Older Dogs

More frequent veterinary visits are advisable for senior dogs. Your vet can monitor for age-related conditions like arthritis, kidney disease, or dental problems. Early detection allows for better management and treatment. Blood tests and regular screenings become more important as dogs age. They can catch issues before they become severe.
