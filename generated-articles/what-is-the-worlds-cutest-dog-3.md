Often, yes. Small dogs frequently rank high in cuteness due to their compact size and delicate features. Their perceived vulnerability can evoke strong feelings of affection. ### Do certain facial features contribute more to a dog's cuteness?
Absolutely. Large, expressive eyes are a prime factor in canine cuteness. Dogs with "smiles" or innocent-looking expressions also appear very charming. Floppy ears or a head tilt can also enhance a dog's adorable appeal. These features make dogs seem more approachable and communicative.

### How much does a dog's personality affect its cuteness?
A dog's personality plays a huge role in its perceived cuteness. A playful, affectionate, or goofy demeanor can make any dog more endearing. While some traits have universal appeal, cuteness can also be culturally influenced. Different cultures might prioritize certain characteristics or breeds.
