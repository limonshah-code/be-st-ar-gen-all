The term "Giant German Shepherd" does not name a distinct, recognized breed. ## The Truth About The "Giant German Shepherd"

Many people ask, "What is the Giant German Shepherd breed?" It is important to know that this term does not describe an official breed. Major kennel clubs like the American Kennel Club (AKC) do not recognize a "Giant German Shepherd." The breed standard for German Shepherds specifies a certain size range. Dogs that are much larger than this standard are not considered a separate breed. They are simply oversized examples of the existing German Shepherd breed.

I have seen many impressive German Shepherds in my life. Some were noticeably larger than others. These big dogs capture attention quickly. Owners often refer to them informally as "giant" because of their impressive stature. However, this large size can come from various factors. Sometimes, it is selective breeding focusing only on size. Other times, it happens due to crossbreeding with other large breeds. These crosses often lead to a bigger dog but do not create a new breed. This leads to common misunderstandings.

Breeders might market "Giant German Shepherds" to attract buyers seeking a truly imposing dog. This can be misleading. A true German Shepherd dog should fit within its breed standard. Deviations often point to health risks. It is crucial to understand the dog's true lineage. This ensures you get a healthy and well-tempered companion. Knowing this truth helps prospective owners make good decisions.

## Breeds Often Mistaken For Giant German Shepherds

The term "Giant German Shepherd" often refers to a few specific breeds. These breeds have German Shepherd ancestors. However, they were developed for a larger size and unique traits. Two main breeds come to mind first. These are the King Shepherd and the Shiloh Shepherd. Each has its own history and characteristics. I find their stories quite fascinating.

### King Shepherd: A Majestic Giant

The King Shepherd is a relatively new breed. It began in the United States in the 1990s. Breeders aimed to create a larger, calmer companion dog. They wanted to keep the intelligence and loyalty of the German Shepherd. This breed results from mixing German Shepherds with other large breeds. These other breeds include the Akita, Alaskan Malamute, and Great Pyrenees. The mix gives the King Shepherd its truly giant size.

King Shepherds are very large dogs. Males can weigh up to 150 pounds and stand 29 inches tall. Females are also substantial, weighing 90-110 pounds and standing 25-27 inches. They have a sturdy, well-muscled body. Their coats are dense and weather-resistant. King Shepherds look like a magnified German Shepherd. Their temperament is generally calm and gentle. They are good with families. This makes them popular. However, they still need proper training and socialization. ### Shiloh Shepherd: A Gentle Protector

The Shiloh Shepherd is another breed often called a "Giant German Shepherd." Tina M. Barber started developing this breed in the 1970s. She wanted a large, intelligent, and healthy dog. She also focused on a softer temperament than some working line German Shepherds. The Shiloh Shepherd comes from specific German Shepherd lines. These lines were bred for size, calm nature, and good health. They also sometimes include a bit of Alaskan Malamute.

Shiloh Shepherds are large dogs. Males usually weigh between 100-140 pounds and stand 28-30 inches tall. Females weigh 80-120 pounds and stand 26-28 inches. They have a thick, soft coat. Their appearance is similar to a German Shepherd but with a broader head and a less sloped back. Shiloh Shepherds are known for their gentle and loyal nature. They are excellent family dogs. They bond strongly with their owners. These dogs need companionship and do not do well when left alone for long periods. They are protective but not aggressive.

## Distinguishing Characteristics Of Large German Shepherd Types

Understanding the unique characteristics helps identify true "Giant German Shepherds." This means recognizing the traits of King Shepherds and Shiloh Shepherds. These dogs share some features with standard German Shepherds. They also have distinct differences due to their breeding. I always look closely at their physical build and personality.

### Physical Appearance And Size

The most obvious difference is size. Standard German Shepherds are large dogs already. Males stand 24-26 inches tall and weigh 65-90 pounds. Females stand 22-24 inches tall and weigh 50-70 pounds. King Shepherds and Shiloh Shepherds go beyond these measurements. King Shepherds are often 27-29 inches tall and weigh 90-150 pounds. Shiloh Shepherds can be 26-30 inches tall and weigh 80-140 pounds. Their size is truly impressive.

Beyond height and weight, look at their build. King Shepherds have a powerful, solid frame. They have deep chests and large bones. Their heads are broad and strong. Shiloh Shepherds have a similar strong build. They often have a softer expression. Their backs are usually less sloped than some show-line German Shepherds. Their coats can be plush or smooth. Color variations are similar to German Shepherds, including black and tan, sable, and solid black. These physical traits help distinguish them.
