**Q3: How do I transition my Golden Retriever to Royal Canin food?**
Transition your dog's food slowly over 7 to 10 days. Start by mixing 25% new Royal Canin food with 75% old food for a few days. Then, move to 50% new and 50% old. Gradually increase the new food ratio until your dog is eating 100% Royal Canin. This helps prevent digestive upset.

**Q4: Does Royal Canin Golden Retriever food help with shedding?**
Royal Canin Golden Retriever formulas are designed to support skin and coat health. They include specific nutrients like omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA). **Q5: What if my Golden Retriever doesn't like the Royal Canin kibble shape?**
The unique kibble shape is designed for the Golden Retriever's jaw. Most dogs adapt well to it. If your dog struggles, ensure they are not eating too fast or have dental issues. You can try moistening the kibble slightly with water initially. If the problem continues, consult your veterinarian.
