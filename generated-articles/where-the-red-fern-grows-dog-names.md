### What does the red fern symbolize in the story?
The red fern in "Where The Red Fern Grows" symbolizes something sacred and magical. Legend says a red fern grows only where an angel has touched the earth. It grows between the graves of Old Dan and Little Ann. It represents their eternal bond and the purity of their love.
