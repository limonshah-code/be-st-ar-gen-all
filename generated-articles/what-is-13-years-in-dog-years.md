### What Is 13 Years In Dog Years?

A 13-year-old dog is a true senior, well into their golden years. The old "7 human years to 1 dog year" rule is inaccurate. Modern science indicates a 13-year-old dog can range from approximately 68 to over 100 human years, depending greatly on their breed and size.
