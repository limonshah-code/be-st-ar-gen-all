### Understanding the Federal Law: The Dog and Cat Meat Trade Prohibition Act

The United States has a clear federal law against the dog and cat meat trade. Congress passed the Dog and Cat Meat Trade Prohibition Act in 2018. This act makes it illegal to slaughter dogs and cats for human consumption. It also prohibits the sale and transport of their meat.

This legislation reflects a widespread sentiment in America. Most Americans view dogs and cats as pets, not food. The law aims to protect these animals. It ensures they are not part of a commercial food supply chain. This act applies across all states and territories.

Before this federal law, individual states had varying regulations. Some states banned dog meat, others did not. The 2018 act created a national standard. It closed loopholes that might have existed.
