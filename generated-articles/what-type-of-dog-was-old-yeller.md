### Common Cur Dog Breeds and Types

While "Cur dog" is a general type, some specific Cur breeds have emerged. These breeds started as localized groups of working dogs. Over time, they gained recognition for consistent traits and abilities. They represent refined versions of the traditional Cur.

*   **Treeing Walker Coonhound:** Known for their speed and ability to "tree" raccoons. These dogs are vocal and agile.
*   **Blackmouth Cur:** A powerful and versatile working dog. They are often used for herding cattle and hunting wild boar.
*   **Mountain Cur:** Smaller than some other Curs, but equally capable. They are excellent hunters of small game and squirrels.
*   **Leopard Cur:** Recognizable by their distinctive spotted coat. They are valued for their hunting prowess and protective nature.

These breeds maintain the core Cur dog qualities of intelligence and loyalty. They have simply been further developed for particular hunting or working styles. The type of dog Old Yeller was most likely a more general, unclassified Cur. He would have possessed the natural instincts and bravery found in these working lineages.
