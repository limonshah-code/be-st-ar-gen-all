Archaeological evidence strongly supports this connection. We have found skeletal remains of small dogs buried with humans in ancient tombs. These findings date back over a thousand years. These dogs shared many similarities with what we now recognize as the Chihuahua. We also see artistic depictions of small dogs in ancient Toltec and Aztec artwork. These images give us clues about their appearance and cultural significance.

## The Evolutionary Path: From Techichi to the Modern Chihuahua
