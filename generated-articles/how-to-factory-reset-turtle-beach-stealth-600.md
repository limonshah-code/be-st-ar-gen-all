Your Turtle Beach Stealth 600 headset is a key part of your gaming setup. Sometimes, performance issues can occur. You might experience sound cutting out, microphone problems, or connection glitches. These problems prevent you from having the best gaming experience.

A factory reset is often the simplest fix for many common headset issues. It restores your headset to its original settings. This action can clear out any software conflicts or corrupted configurations. We will show you exactly how to factory reset your Turtle Beach Stealth 600. This guide helps you get your headset working perfectly again.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Factory Reset Turtle Beach Stealth 600

Now we will guide you through the exact steps to factory reset your Turtle Beach Stealth 600. This method applies to both Gen 1 and Gen 2 versions of the headset. Follow these instructions carefully. It will restore your headset to its default settings.

First, power on your Turtle Beach Stealth 600 headset. Ensure it has a full charge. This is a critical step, as mentioned before. Hold the headset in your hand, ready to press the required buttons.

Next, locate the 'MODE' button and the 'POWER' button on your headset. The 'MODE' button is usually on the left earcup. The 'POWER' button is also on the left earcup, typically closer to the bottom. These are the two buttons you need for the reset.

Press and hold both the 'MODE' button and the 'POWER' button simultaneously. Keep holding them down. Continue to hold them for about 20 seconds. During this time, the headset's LED indicator might flash. It may also change colors. Do not release the buttons until you see the headset power off.

After approximately 20 seconds, the headset will power off. Then, it will automatically power back on. This sequence confirms that the factory reset is complete. The LED indicator will light up again, signaling it is ready. You have now successfully restored your Turtle Beach Stealth 600 to its original state.

### Re-Pairing Your Headset After a Reset

A factory reset clears all previous pairings from your Turtle Beach Stealth 600. This means your headset will not automatically connect to your gaming device. You must re-pair it with its USB transmitter. This is an essential step after any factory reset.

First, connect the USB transmitter to your console or PC. Ensure it is securely plugged into an available USB port. The transmitter's LED indicator will start flashing. This indicates it is in pairing mode. It is waiting for a connection from your headset.

Next, power on your Turtle Beach Stealth 600 headset. You just performed a factory reset, so it should be in a fresh state. Press and hold the 'POWER' button on the headset for a few seconds. The headset's LED will also begin flashing rapidly. This means your headset is also searching for a connection.

Place the headset close to the transmitter. Allow them a few moments to establish a connection. Both the headset and transmitter LEDs will eventually turn solid. A solid light confirms a successful pairing. You have now reconnected your headset.

If you are trying to connect to a different device or had issues with the original pairing, this step is crucial. Sometimes, you might even need to [pair Turtle Beach Stealth 600 to a new transmitter](https://beacleaner.com/how-to-pair-turtle-beach-stealth-600-to-new-transmitter) if the old one is lost or faulty. Once paired, test your audio and microphone. Make sure everything works as expected.
