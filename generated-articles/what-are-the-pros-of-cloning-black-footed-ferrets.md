### Q4: What is a genetic bottleneck, and how does cloning help address it?

A genetic bottleneck occurs when a population shrinks dramatically. This reduces genetic diversity. The black-footed ferret population went through a severe bottleneck. Cloning helps by reintroducing lost genetic material. Scientists use DNA from individuals that existed before the bottleneck. This infusion of new genes expands the gene pool.

### Q5: How does cloning improve disease resistance in ferrets?

Cloning can improve disease resistance by reintroducing specific genes. If an archived ferret had natural immunity to a disease like sylvatic plague, its DNA can be used. ### Q6: Are cloned black-footed ferrets exactly like naturally born ferrets?

Cloned ferrets are genetically identical to their donor. However, they are not exact copies in every way. Environmental factors during development also play a role.
