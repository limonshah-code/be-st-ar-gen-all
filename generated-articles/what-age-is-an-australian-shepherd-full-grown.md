### Physical Maturity: When Aussie Body Stops Growing

The first major stage is rapid bone growth, primarily in height. This occurs from birth up to around 12 to 16 months. During this time, the growth plates in their bones are open. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do Australian Shepherds stop growing in height?
Australian Shepherds typically stop growing in height between 12 and 16 months of age. At this point, their growth plates have usually closed. While they may not get taller, they will continue to fill out. This means gaining muscle and weight. This phase extends into their second year.

### Do female Australian Shepherds grow faster than males?
Female Australian Shepherds often mature slightly faster than males. They may reach their full height and weight a bit earlier. However, the difference is usually only a few months. Both sexes generally follow similar growth patterns. Females are also typically smaller overall than males.

### What are signs my Aussie is still growing?
Signs your Aussie is still growing include disproportionate body parts, like large paws or a lean, gangly appearance. They will also gain weight steadily. Continued height increases and a general appearance of not being "filled out" are also clues. You might also notice their energy levels are very high, typical of adolescents.

### Can diet affect how big my Australian Shepherd gets?
Yes, diet significantly affects your Australian Shepherd's growth. A high-quality puppy food specifically for medium or large breeds supports proper development. Overfeeding or underfeeding can impact their final size and health. It can lead to obesity or stunted growth. A balanced diet ensures healthy bone and muscle development.

### Is an Australian Shepherd full grown at 1 year?
An Australian Shepherd is usually full grown in height by 1 year. However, they are not completely finished growing. They will continue to fill out, gaining muscle mass and weight, until they are 18 to 24 months old. Mentally, they are still maturing well past their first birthday.

### What is the average full-grown weight for an Australian Shepherd?
A full-grown male Australian Shepherd typically weighs between 50-65 pounds. Females usually weigh 40-55 pounds. These weights can vary based on genetics, diet, and activity level. Always aim for a healthy weight for your individual dog. Consult your vet for precise guidance.
