### Key Techniques for Competitive Eaters

Competitive eaters use several key techniques. The "Solomon Method" is a famous one. This involves separating the hot dog from the bun. Eaters dunk the bun in water. This makes it a soggy, manageable mass. They eat the hot dog quickly. Then, they eat the bun. They do this in quick alternating movements.

Another technique is "chipmunking." This involves stuffing food into the cheeks. They do this just before the clock runs out. This maximizes the final count. They also focus on constant chewing and swallowing. This avoids pauses. Eaters train to reduce jaw fatigue. These methods improve their performance significantly.
