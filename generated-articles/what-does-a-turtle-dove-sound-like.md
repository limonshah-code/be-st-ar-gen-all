## Distinguishing Turtle Dove Sounds from Other Doves

Identifying a Turtle Dove by its sound requires careful listening. Other dove species make similar cooing sounds. However, each species has distinct features in its vocalization. It helps to know these differences. This way, you can correctly identify the bird you hear.

**Key Differences in Dove Calls:**

*   **Turtle Dove:** Its call is a soft, continuous "turrr-turrr-turrr." It is a gentle purring. The sound has a smooth, flowing rhythm. It lacks distinct syllables.
*   **Mourning Dove:** This dove has a mournful coo. It sounds like "hoo-AH-hoo-hoo-hoo." The first note is usually higher. It has a sad, drawn-out quality. This is very different from the Turtle Dove's purr.
*   **Eurasian Collared-Dove:** Their call is a three-note coo: "hoo-HOO-hoo." The middle note is emphasized. It is more robust than the Turtle Dove's sound. This call is also quite repetitive.
*   **Stock Dove:** This dove has a very deep, booming "oooo-ooo-ooo" call. It is often described as slightly hooting. Its sound is much lower in pitch than a Turtle Dove's.
*   **Wood Pigeon:** These birds have a well-known five-note call. It sounds like "coo-COO-coo-coo-coo." The sound is loud and can be heard far away. It is much more varied than a Turtle Dove's single purring note.
