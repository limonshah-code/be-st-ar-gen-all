### Methods of Measuring Bite Force

Measuring the bite force of large, dangerous animals is a complex task. Scientists typically use specialized tools called force transducers or bite plates. These devices are equipped with sensors that record the pressure exerted by an animal's jaws. For wild animals, researchers sometimes lure the animal to bite a sensor. This requires careful planning and safety protocols.

In some cases, researchers use anatomical models. They measure muscle size and jaw mechanics. They then calculate the theoretical bite force. While these methods provide accurate data, direct measurement remains the gold standard. Ensuring the safety of both researchers and animals is always the top priority.
