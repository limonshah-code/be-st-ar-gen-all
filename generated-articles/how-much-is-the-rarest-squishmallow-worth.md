## Authenticating Your Collectible Squishmallows

When dealing with rare and valuable Squishmallows, authenticity is very important. Fake Squishmallows exist in the market. Knowing how to spot a real one protects your investment. It ensures you pay for a genuine collectible. Checking a few key details helps confirm legitimacy.

### Checking Tags and Labels

Every authentic Squishmallow has specific tags. Look for the hang tag, which is the paper tag attached to its ear or body. This tag usually has the Squishmallow's name, biography, and collector number. Also, check the tush tag, which is sewn into the Squishmallow itself. This fabric tag shows production details, materials, and safety standards. Both tags should be clear, correct, and firmly attached. Mismatched fonts or blurry printing can be red flags. Missing tags often lower a Squishmallow's value, even if it is rare.

### Condition and Care

The overall condition of a Squishmallow greatly affects its worth. A truly valuable item will be in perfect, like-new condition. This means no stains, rips, or fading. The plush material should feel soft and firm. It should not be matted or squashed. Proper storage prevents damage from dust, sunlight, and everyday wear. Keeping original tags intact and clean also adds to the value. A Squishmallow with pristine condition and all its tags will always fetch a higher price.
