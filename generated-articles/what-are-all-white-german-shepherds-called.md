### What Are All White German Shepherds Called?

All white German Shepherds are primarily known as the Berger Blanc Suisse. This name translates to "White Swiss Shepherd." They are recognized as a distinct breed in many parts of the world. While they share ancestry with German Shepherds, they represent a separate lineage.

## The Official Name: Berger Blanc Suisse / White Swiss Shepherd

When we talk about pure white dogs resembling German Shepherds, we are talking about the Berger Blanc Suisse. This is the official breed name. It means "White Swiss Shepherd" in French. This breed gained official recognition in many international kennel clubs. It happened much later than the German Shepherd Dog.

The Federation Cynologique Internationale (FCI) recognized the Berger Blanc Suisse in 2003. This recognition marked a significant step. It distinguished them from the standard German Shepherd. The breed standard now defines their appearance, temperament, and health. This official status helps protect the breed's integrity. It also promotes responsible breeding practices.

I find this official recognition important. It helps avoid confusion about their origins. It clarifies that they are not just German Shepherds with a different coat color. They are a separate, unique breed with their own specific characteristics. This clarity ensures breeders work towards established goals. It also educates the public about these wonderful dogs.

Many breed clubs worldwide now support the Berger Blanc Suisse. These clubs promote health testing and responsible ownership. They work to maintain the breed's excellent temperament. This strong network helps the breed thrive. It ensures future generations of these beautiful dogs.

## A Brief History of the White Coat in GSDs

The story of the white coat begins with the original German Shepherd Dog. The early German Shepherd Dog lines included white-coated members. This white color came from a recessive gene. It was always present in the breed's genetic makeup. These dogs were not albino. They simply carried a gene for a white coat.

Captain Max von Stephanitz founded the German Shepherd breed. He emphasized utility and working ability. Initially, coat color was not a disqualifying factor. White German Shepherds were part of the foundational stock. They contributed to the breed's development. This is a fact many people do not know.

Over time, breed standards evolved. The German Shepherd Dog Club of Germany eventually declared white coats undesirable. This decision happened in the early 20th century. White-coated puppies were excluded from official German Shepherd registration. This led to a split in the breed's lineage. White dogs could not compete or breed within the established GSD lines.

Despite this exclusion, white German Shepherds continued to exist. Enthusiasts in North America preserved these dogs. They recognized their qualities. They appreciated their unique beauty. Breeders actively worked to maintain these lines. They sought to establish them as a separate breed. Their efforts saved the white-coated lineage. The original German Shepherd Dog breed showed a wide range of characteristics, including different sizes and [builds among its members](https://beacleaner.com/what-are-the-biggest-german-shepherds-called). This preservation journey eventually led to the official recognition of the Berger Blanc Suisse.

## Health Considerations for White-Coated Dogs

The health of White Swiss Shepherds is generally good. Like all purebred dogs, they have some predispositions. It is important for owners to know these. Responsible breeding practices help minimize risks. I always recommend thorough health testing for parents.

### Common Health Issues

Some conditions seen in German Shepherds can also affect White Swiss Shepherds. These include hip and elbow dysplasia. These are common joint problems. Breeders should screen for these conditions. Reputable breeders test their dogs before breeding them. This helps ensure healthy puppies.

Degenerative Myelopathy (DM) is another concern. This is a progressive neurological disease. It affects the spinal cord. There is a DNA test available for DM. Breeding parents should be tested for this condition. This allows breeders to make informed decisions. It reduces the chance of passing on the gene.

### Skin and Allergy Concerns

White-coated dogs sometimes have sensitive skin. They can be prone to allergies. These allergies can be environmental or food-related. Symptoms include itching, redness, or ear infections. Regular grooming helps monitor their skin health. A high-quality diet also supports a healthy coat and skin.

Sunburn is a risk for any dog with a lighter coat or pink skin. White Swiss Shepherds have dark pigment. This offers some protection. Still, prolonged sun exposure can be harmful. I always ensure my dogs have shade when outside. Applying dog-safe sunscreen to sensitive areas, like the nose, can be helpful.

### Not Albino

It is crucial to understand that White Swiss Shepherds are not albinos. Albinism is a genetic condition. It results in a complete lack of pigment. Albino dogs have pink noses, pink paw pads, and blue or pink eyes. They also have severe health issues. These include extreme light sensitivity and poor vision. White Swiss Shepherds have dark eyes, noses, and paw pads. This means they have pigment. Their white coat is due to a recessive gene. This gene hides other colors. They do not suffer from the health problems associated with albinism.

## FAQ Section

### Are White German Shepherds recognized by the AKC?
No, the American Kennel Club (AKC) does not recognize white-coated German Shepherds. The AKC considers a white coat a disqualifying fault for the German Shepherd Dog breed standard. ### What causes a German Shepherd to be white?
A white coat in these dogs comes from a recessive gene. This gene hides other colors, resulting in a pure white appearance. It is a natural color variation. These dogs are not albinos. They possess normal pigmentation in their eyes, nose, and paw pads. This genetic trait has been present in German Shepherd lineage from its earliest days.

### Is the Berger Blanc Suisse a rare breed?

### How much exercise do White Swiss Shepherds need?
