## Safety Best Practices and Professionalism for Wag Walkers

As a Wag walker, safety must always be your top priority. This applies to the dogs you care for, yourself, and the general public. Wag provides guidelines. It is your responsibility to follow them diligently. Professionalism builds trust. It ensures a positive experience for everyone involved.

I have found that a proactive approach to safety prevents most issues. Being prepared and aware helps you handle unexpected situations. This dedication to safety helps you become a successful dog walker on Wag.

### Prioritizing Pet Safety

*   **Leash Security:** Always double-check the leash and harness before starting a walk. Make sure they are secure. A slipped leash is a major safety concern.
*   **Environmental Awareness:** Pay attention to your surroundings. Avoid broken glass, toxic plants, or other hazards. Be mindful of traffic and other dogs.
*   **Weather Conditions:** Adjust walk times or duration based on the weather. Hot pavement can burn paws. Extreme cold is dangerous. Offer indoor play or shorter walks when weather is severe.
*   **Hydration:** Carry water, especially on longer walks or warm days. Offer the dog water regularly.
*   **Dog Temperament:** Understand the dog's behavior. If a dog shows aggression towards other dogs or people, take appropriate action. Keep them at a safe distance. Follow the owner's instructions regarding other dogs.

### Handling Emergencies

Wag provides emergency support. Familiarize yourself with the app's emergency features. Know how to contact the pet owner and Wag support quickly. If a dog becomes ill or injured, act fast. Seek veterinary care if necessary.

Always carry your phone during walks. Make sure it is charged. This ensures you can call for help if an emergency occurs. Remain calm during an emergency. Clear thinking helps you make better decisions.

### Client Communication Etiquette

Maintain open and respectful communication with pet parents. Respond to messages promptly. Provide clear, honest updates. If you anticipate a delay, inform the owner immediately. This shows respect for their time.

Avoid oversharing personal information. Keep conversations focused on the pet and the service. Professional boundaries are important. They help maintain a trusting relationship.

### Maintaining a Positive Attitude

A positive attitude makes a big difference. Dogs respond well to calm and happy energy. Pet owners also appreciate a friendly and enthusiastic walker. Approach each walk with enthusiasm. Be ready to give the dog a great experience.
