### What is the meaning behind the name "Flo & Eddie"?
The names "Flo" (Phlorescent Leech) and "Eddie" were given to Mark Volman and Howard Kaylan, respectively, by Frank Zappa. They adopted these playful monikers when they were legally barred from using "The Turtles" name. The names stuck throughout their subsequent careers.
