## Caring for Your Ace Squishmallow: Keeping Your Plush Pal Pristine

Once you find your beloved Ace, caring for him ensures he stays soft and huggable. Proper maintenance helps keep your Squishmallow in excellent condition. These plush toys are known for their marshmallow-like texture. Protecting this softness is key. I always take good care of my Squishmallows.

Squishmallows are generally easy to clean. Most can be machine washed on a gentle cycle. Use cold water and place Ace in a pillowcase or laundry bag. This protects his delicate fabric and stuffing. Avoid using harsh detergents or bleach, as these can damage the material.

After washing, air drying is the best method. You can tumble dry on a very low heat setting if necessary. However, excessive heat can melt the fibers or affect the plush's softness. Allow Ace to fully dry before cuddling him again. This prevents mildew and keeps him fresh.

### Tips for Maintaining Ace's Softness and Shape:

*   **Spot Clean for Small Stains:** For minor spills, use a damp cloth and mild soap. Gently dab the stained area. Avoid rubbing vigorously, as this can spread the stain or damage the fabric.
*   **Fluff Regularly:** Squishmallows can get a bit flat over time. Gently patting and fluffing Ace helps redistribute his stuffing. This brings back his original plump shape.
*   **Store Properly:** Keep Ace away from direct sunlight, which can fade colors. Store him in a clean, dry place. Avoid placing heavy items on top of him, which could permanently compress the stuffing.
*   **Avoid Excessive Washing:** While washable, frequent washing can wear down the fabric. Only wash Ace when truly necessary. Spot cleaning is often sufficient for light dirt.

By following these simple care instructions, your Ace the Australian Shepherd Squishmallow will remain a cherished companion. He will keep his soft texture and vibrant colors for many years. A well-cared-for Squishmallow brings lasting comfort and joy.
