### FAQ Section

**Are Boston Terrier puppies very hyper?**
Yes, Boston Terrier puppies are typically very energetic and playful. They have a lot of curiosity and need plenty of short play sessions throughout the day. Early training and socialization are important to help them channel their energy in positive ways as they grow.

**How much exercise does a French Bulldog need to not be hyper?**
French Bulldogs need moderate exercise. Two short, leisurely walks of 15-20 minutes each day are usually enough. Over-exercising them can be dangerous due to their breathing issues. Mental stimulation from puzzle toys or short training sessions also helps keep them calm.

**Can a French Bulldog be hyper?**
French Bulldogs can have bursts of energy, especially as puppies or when excited. However, they are not typically considered "hyper" in the same way a Boston Terrier might be. Their hyperactivity is usually short-lived and followed by rest. Always watch for signs of overheating during play.

**How do I calm down a hyper Boston Terrier?**
To calm a hyper Boston Terrier, ensure they get enough daily physical exercise and mental stimulation. Consistent training helps them learn commands like "settle." Provide plenty of appropriate chew toys. A quiet space for rest and relaxation is also important for them to unwind.

**What are signs of a happy vs. hyper Boston Terrier?**
A happy Boston Terrier is playful, engaged, and responsive. A hyper one might be restless, destructive, or excessively barking without clear reason. Hyperactivity often stems from boredom or lack of exercise. Meeting their needs helps differentiate a happy, energetic dog from an overly hyper one.
