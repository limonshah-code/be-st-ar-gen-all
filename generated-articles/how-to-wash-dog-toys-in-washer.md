## Wash Dog Toys in Your Washer: A Complete Guide

Your dog's toys bring joy, but they also collect dirt, slobber, and germs. Regular cleaning is important for your pet's health and your home's hygiene. You can effectively wash many dog toys right in your washing machine. This guide makes the process simple and safe for all kinds of toys.

I know how much our furry friends love their toys. Keeping them clean is a task many pet parents face. Dirty toys can harbor bacteria and odors. Washing them helps keep your dog healthy and their play area fresh. This article will show you exactly how to wash dog toys in your washer. We cover proper preparation, washing different toy types, and safe drying methods.

### Takeaway

*   Always check toy labels for washing instructions.
*   Pre-treat heavily soiled toys before machine washing.
*   Use a mesh laundry bag to protect smaller items.
*   Choose gentle, pet-safe detergents.
*   Air dry or use low heat to prevent damage.

### How to Wash Dog Toys in Washer: Quick Answer

To wash dog toys in a washer, first check labels and remove loose debris. Place toys in a mesh laundry bag. Use a gentle cycle with cold water and a pet-safe detergent. Air dry or tumble dry on low heat, ensuring toys are fully dry before returning to your dog.

### Preparing Dog Toys for Machine Washing

Before you wash dog toys in the washer, some preparation is key. This step helps protect the toys and your washing machine. Proper preparation ensures a better cleaning outcome. It also prevents any damage to delicate items.

First, remove all visible dirt and debris. Shake off loose hair or dirt. You can also use a stiff brush to scrub off caked-on mud. For tougher stains, pre-treat them with a pet-safe stain remover. This initial cleaning saves your washing machine from excess grime.

Next, inspect each toy for damage. Look for tears, loose stuffing, or small parts that could come off. Repair any minor damage if possible. Discard toys that are too worn or broken. These items could further break apart in the wash.

Separate toys by material and color. This prevents color bleeding and allows you to choose the right wash cycle. Small toys and those with delicate parts should go into a [mesh laundry bag](https://beacleaner.com/what-is-the-best-way-to-clean-plush-toys). This bag protects them from getting lost or damaged. It also helps contain any stuffing that might escape.

### Choosing the Right Detergent for Dog Toys

Selecting the correct detergent is very important when you wash dog toys in the washer. Your dog will put these toys in their mouth. Harsh chemicals can be harmful to their health. Always prioritize pet-safe options.

Standard laundry detergents often contain strong fragrances, dyes, and chemicals. These can irritate your dog's skin or stomach. Residues left on toys could cause problems if ingested. It's better to avoid these products when cleaning pet items.

Look for detergents labeled as "free and clear" or "hypoallergenic." These detergents typically lack perfumes, dyes, and other common irritants. Some brands specifically formulate detergents for pet laundry. These are excellent choices for washing dog toys. I often opt for plant-based detergents myself.

You can also use a small amount of distilled white vinegar. Vinegar acts as a natural disinfectant and deodorizer. Add half a cup to the rinse cycle for extra cleaning power. It's safe for pets and leaves no harmful residue. Avoid using bleach unless absolutely necessary and follow dilution instructions carefully. Bleach can be very dangerous for animals.

### Washing Plush and Fabric Dog Toys

Plush and fabric toys are favorites among many dogs. These soft toys are often machine washable. Knowing how to properly wash dog toys in the washer ensures they stay fluffy and clean. [How often should you wash dog toys](https://beacleaner.com/how-often-should-you-wash-dog-toys) like these depends on how much your dog uses them. I usually wash my dog's plush toys once a week.

Before washing, check the toy's tag for specific instructions. Most plush toys can handle a gentle cycle. Place them inside a mesh laundry bag. This protects the toys from snags and helps keep their shape. You can put multiple small toys in one bag.

Use cold water for the wash cycle. Cold water helps prevent colors from fading and fabric from shrinking. Select the "delicate" or "gentle" cycle setting. A mild, pet-safe detergent is essential. Use only a small amount. Too much detergent can leave residue.

For toys with squeakers, check if they can withstand water. Many modern squeakers are designed to be water-resistant. However, some may stop working after a wash. If a toy has electronics, remove them before washing if possible. If not, it's best to hand wash that particular toy.

### Cleaning Rubber and Hard Plastic Dog Toys

Rubber and hard plastic toys are very durable. These types of toys are generally easy to clean in the washing machine. Knowing the right method helps keep them hygienic. My dog loves his tough rubber chew toys, and they get very dirty.

Start by scrubbing off any visible dirt or gunk. A stiff brush works well for textured surfaces. For deeply embedded dirt, a strong stream of water can help. Place these toys in a mesh laundry bag. This prevents them from clanking around loudly in the machine. It also protects your washer's drum from scratches. You can wash several rubber or plastic toys together.

Use a normal or heavy-duty cycle for these robust toys. Cold or warm water works well. Hot water can sometimes deform certain plastics or rubbers. Use a pet-safe detergent. For extra sanitization, you can add half a cup of white vinegar to the wash. This helps kill bacteria and remove odors.

After the wash, inspect the toys. Make sure no water is trapped inside hollow toys. Shake them out thoroughly. These toys air dry relatively quickly. [How to clean plastic toys](https://beacleaner.com/how-to-clean-plastic-toys) effectively is key to preventing mold.

### Disinfecting Dog Toys in the Washer

Disinfecting dog toys is crucial, especially if your dog has been sick. The washing machine can help sanitize many types of toys. This step ensures germs are eliminated. Regular disinfection helps maintain a healthy environment for your pet.

First, always check if the toy material can handle disinfection. Most fabric, rubber, and hard plastic toys are fine. Toys with batteries or electronics should not go into the washer for disinfection. You should also consider [how do you disinfect toys after being sick](https://beacleaner.com/how-do-you-disinfect-toys-after-being-sick) for your human family members too.

For fabric and plush toys, use a gentle, bleach-free disinfectant. Some pet-safe laundry additives are designed for this purpose. You can add them directly to the wash cycle. White vinegar is also an effective natural option. Add it during the rinse cycle.

For rubber and hard plastic toys, a normal wash cycle with hot water (if safe for the material) and vinegar can be very effective. Ensure the toys are well-rinsed. Hot water helps kill bacteria. If you use a diluted bleach solution, always rinse the toys multiple times. This removes all traces of bleach.

After washing, ensure all toys are completely dry. Moisture can lead to mold growth. This defeats the purpose of disinfection. Always prioritize pet safety when choosing disinfectants.

### Drying Dog Toys Safely After Washing

Proper drying is just as important as washing. Incorrect drying can damage toys or encourage mold growth. This is the final step to ensuring clean, safe toys for your dog. I always make sure toys are completely dry.

For most fabric and plush toys, air drying is the best method. Hang them on a clothesline or lay them flat on a clean surface. Ensure good air circulation. You can also use a laundry rack. This helps prevent mildew and preserves the toy's shape.

If you use a dryer, select the lowest heat setting. High heat can melt plastics, shrink fabrics, or damage delicate components. Add a few dry towels to the dryer. This helps absorb moisture faster and prevents toys from clumping. Check toys frequently for dryness.

Rubber and hard plastic toys typically air dry quickly. You can place them in a well-ventilated area. Sunlight can help speed up the process and offer a natural sanitizing effect. Ensure there is no standing water inside hollow toys. Water can lead to mold.

Never return a damp toy to your dog. Even slightly damp toys can harbor bacteria or mold. This could cause skin irritations or other health issues. Patience is key for thorough drying.

Washing dog toys in the washer can leave behind pet hair and dirt. Keeping your washing machine clean is important for its longevity. It also ensures your clothes stay clean after washing pet items. I always make sure to clean my washer after a load of dog toys.

After washing dog toys, remove any loose pet hair from the washer drum. You can use a damp cloth or a vacuum attachment for this. Pet hair can clog drains or stick to your next load of laundry. Performing these steps regularly keeps your washer fresh and ready for all your laundry needs. A clean machine means cleaner clothes and toys. It also helps prevent unpleasant odors from lingering.

### When to Hand Wash Dog Toys Instead of Using a Washer

While washing dog toys in the washer is convenient, some toys require hand washing. Certain materials or designs are not suitable for machine agitation. Knowing when to hand wash protects your dog's toys from damage. It also prevents potential machine problems.

*   **Toys with batteries or electronics:** These should never go into a washing machine. Water can permanently damage internal components. Wipe these toys clean with a damp cloth and a pet-safe disinfectant spray.
*   **Toys with delicate embellishments:** Small glued-on parts, intricate stitching, or delicate accessories may not survive a machine wash. Hand wash these gently to preserve their appearance.
*   **Toys made from non-machine-washable materials:** Some materials, like certain types of foam or wood, are not designed for machine washing. Always check the care label.
*   **Very old or extremely fragile toys:** If a toy is already falling apart, the washing machine will likely accelerate its destruction. Hand washing is a gentler option for these sentimental items.
*   **Large, bulky toys that don't fit well:** Overloading your washing machine can prevent toys from getting clean. It can also strain the machine. For very large items, hand washing in a tub might be more practical.

Hand washing allows for more control over the cleaning process. It helps you focus on specific dirty spots. Use a basin with warm water and a small amount of pet-safe soap. Rinse thoroughly and air dry completely.

### How Often Should You Wash Dog Toys?

The frequency of washing dog toys depends on several factors. This includes how often your dog plays with them and the type of toy. Regular cleaning is important for hygiene and health. I typically wash my dog's toys weekly.

For frequently used toys, especially soft ones, aim for at least once a week. These toys collect a lot of saliva, dirt, and bacteria quickly. If your dog carries them outside or shares them with other dogs, more frequent washing is better. This helps prevent the spread of germs.

Harder toys, like rubber or plastic ones, can often go a bit longer. Clean these every 2-4 weeks. If they are heavily soiled or smell bad, wash them sooner. Toys that your dog mouths constantly should be cleaned more often.

If your dog has been sick, wash all their toys immediately. This helps prevent re-infection and protects other pets in the home. Disinfecting during this time is especially important. ### FAQs

**Q: Can I use regular laundry detergent to wash dog toys?**
A: It is not recommended to use regular laundry detergent. These often contain harsh chemicals, dyes, and fragrances. These ingredients can irritate your dog's skin or be harmful if ingested. Opt for pet-safe, unscented, hypoallergenic, or "free and clear" detergents for your dog's toys.

**Q: Do I need to put dog toys in a laundry bag before washing?**
A: Yes, using a mesh laundry bag is highly recommended. It protects smaller toys from getting lost or damaged. It also prevents delicate parts from snagging. For hard plastic or rubber toys, it reduces noise and protects your washing machine drum.

**Q: What washing machine setting is best for dog toys?**
A: For most dog toys, especially plush and fabric ones, use a "delicate" or "gentle" cycle with cold water. For durable rubber or hard plastic toys, a "normal" cycle with cold or warm water is usually fine. Always check the toy's care label if available.

**Q: How do I remove stubborn odors from dog toys?**
A: To remove stubborn odors, add half a cup of white distilled vinegar to the rinse cycle. Vinegar is a natural deodorizer and disinfectant. For very strong odors, you can pre-soak the toys in a solution of warm water and vinegar before machine washing.

**Q: Can I put all types of dog toys in the dryer?**
A: No, not all types of dog toys should go in the dryer. Plush and fabric toys can be tumble dried on a very low heat setting or air dried. Rubber and plastic toys are best air-dried to prevent melting or warping. Always ensure toys are completely dry to prevent mold growth.

**Q: Is it safe to wash dog toys with my regular laundry?**
A: It's generally not recommended to wash dog toys with your regular laundry. Dog toys can carry a lot of dirt, hair, and bacteria. It's best to wash them separately to prevent contamination and ensure they get a thorough cleaning without transferring germs to your clothes.
