## FAQ Section

### What documents do I need for a dog license?
You typically need proof of your dog's current rabies vaccination. This is a certificate from your veterinarian. If your dog is spayed or neutered, a certificate for that procedure helps reduce fees. You will also need your own identification and proof of address.

### How much does a dog license cost?
The cost varies greatly by location. Most licenses range from $10 to $60 per year. There are often discounts for spayed or neutered dogs. Some areas also offer reduced fees for senior citizens or individuals with disabilities. Check your local animal control website for exact prices.

### Can I get a dog license online?
Many cities and counties offer online dog license applications. Visit your local animal control or city/county government website. Look for a "pet licensing" or "animal services" section. You can usually upload documents and pay fees directly through their online portal.

### What happens if I do not license my dog?
Not licensing your dog can lead to fines. It is a legal requirement in most places. If your unlicensed dog gets lost, it is harder for shelters to identify you. Your dog could also be impounded, leading to additional fees for impoundment and boarding.

### How often do I need to renew my dog's license?
Most dog licenses require annual renewal. Some jurisdictions offer multi-year options for convenience. You will typically receive a reminder notice before your license expires. Make sure your dog's rabies vaccination is current when you renew.

### Are service dogs exempt from license fees?
Yes, service dogs are generally exempt from standard pet licensing fees. They still need to register in most areas. This helps identify them as working animals. You will likely need to provide documentation of their service animal status. Emotional support animals usually require a standard pet license and fee.
