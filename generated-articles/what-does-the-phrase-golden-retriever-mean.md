## The Origin: The Dog Behind the Phrase

Before we discuss people, we must acknowledge the dogs themselves. Golden Retrievers are a specific dog breed, cherished worldwide. They originated in Scotland during the 19th century, bred for retrieving game. Their history is rich with purpose and companionship.

The breed quickly became famous for its friendly disposition and intelligence. They are known for being excellent family pets and capable working dogs. This natural temperament laid the groundwork for the human comparison. People observe these dogs and associate their traits with certain human behaviors.

Breed characteristics include a gentle mouth, a strong desire to please, and high energy. Golden Retrievers love people and thrive on interaction. Their inherent good nature makes them a symbol of warmth and loyalty. This deep connection to the breed's character is central to the phrase's meaning.
