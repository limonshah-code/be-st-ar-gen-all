## Witnessing Sea Turtle Hatchings on Jekyll Island

Imagine tiny sea turtles scurrying across the sand, racing towards the vast ocean. This incredible natural event unfolds each year on Georgia’s beautiful Jekyll Island. This barrier island serves as a vital nesting ground for these ancient mariners. Many people ask, "When do sea turtles hatch on Jekyll Island?" Understanding this timing helps us appreciate and protect these vulnerable creatures.

In this article, we will explore the precise period for sea turtle hatching. We will also look at the amazing journey hatchlings undertake. You will learn about the main species that nests here and how you can observe them without causing harm. Join me as we uncover the magic of sea turtle hatching on Jekyll Island. This guide helps you prepare for a responsible and memorable wildlife encounter.
