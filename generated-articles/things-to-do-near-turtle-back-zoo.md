## Discover South Mountain Reservation's Natural Beauty

Right next to Turtle Back Zoo lies the vast expanse of South Mountain Reservation. This beautiful natural park offers a perfect escape into nature. It provides an ideal complement to your zoo visit. Many people enjoy walking or running on its trails. I find the fresh air and green surroundings truly relaxing.

The reservation covers over 2,100 acres. It features dense forests, waterfalls, and scenic overlooks. You can spend hours here without seeing the same spot twice. It is one of the top **things to do near Turtle Back Zoo** for nature lovers. Families often bring picnic baskets for a meal amidst the trees.

### Explore Hiking Trails and Scenic Views

South Mountain Reservation boasts miles of well-maintained hiking trails. These trails vary in difficulty, suiting both casual strollers and serious hikers. You can choose a short loop for a quick walk or a longer path for a more challenging experience. My favorite trails lead to impressive views of the New York City skyline. These viewpoints provide incredible photo opportunities.

The trails are clearly marked. This makes navigation simple. Hikers often spot local wildlife, like deer or various bird species. Remember to wear comfortable shoes and bring water. A hike here is a fantastic way to connect with nature.

### Enjoy Family Picnics and Playgrounds

Families frequently visit South Mountain Reservation for its excellent picnic spots. There are numerous designated areas with picnic tables and open spaces. The reservation also features several playgrounds. These play areas provide swings, slides, and climbing structures. Children love burning off energy in a safe environment. ## Thrilling Adventures at Treetop Adventure Course

For those seeking a bit more excitement, the Essex County Treetop Adventure Course is a must-visit. This outdoor aerial park sits conveniently next to the Turtle Back Zoo entrance. It offers a unique and active experience. I always recommend this place for families looking for an adrenaline rush. It is certainly one of the most exciting **things to do near Turtle Back Zoo**.

The course provides various challenges suspended high in the trees. Participants wear harnesses and helmets for safety. Trained staff guide you through the process. It is an activity that builds confidence and creates lasting memories. You will feel a sense of accomplishment after finishing the course.

### Conquer Obstacles for All Skill Levels

The Treetop Adventure Course features multiple courses designed for different ages and skill levels. There's a junior course for younger children. This course has lower obstacles and easier challenges. It allows little ones to join the fun safely. The main course offers more advanced obstacles. These include rope bridges, balance beams, and climbing nets.

Each section presents a new test of agility and strength. You move from tree to tree, tackling each challenge. It is a fantastic way to push your limits in a safe setting. Everyone can find a course that suits them. This makes it an inclusive adventure for the whole family.

### Experience Ziplining Excitement

One of the highlights of the Treetop Adventure Course is its thrilling ziplines. After completing various obstacles, you get to soar through the air. The ziplines provide a fast and exhilarating ride. They offer a unique perspective of the surrounding forest. The feeling of gliding through the trees is truly unforgettable.

Some courses end with a long zipline, offering a spectacular finish. It is a great reward after conquering the physical challenges. The ziplines are secure and regularly inspected. They add an extra layer of excitement to your adventure. This course truly offers a full package of aerial fun.

## Family Entertainment at Codey Arena and Roller Skating

Adjacent to the zoo complex, you will find the Richard J. Codey Arena. This multi-purpose facility offers year-round fun. It provides excellent options for family entertainment. Many locals consider it a prime spot for active recreation. I always enjoy a good skate session here. It is another great item on the list of **things to do near Turtle Back Zoo**.

The arena hosts a variety of events. It is primarily known for its ice-skating rink. However, during warmer months, it transforms for roller skating. Codey Arena features two NHL-sized ice rinks. They host public skating sessions regularly. This allows visitors of all ages to enjoy ice skating. You can rent skates on-site, making it easy to join. Beginners often appreciate the supportive atmosphere. Experienced skaters can practice their moves.

Public skating is a healthy and enjoyable activity for the entire family. It helps improve balance and coordination. The arena provides a comfortable environment. It is well-maintained and clean. Check their schedule online for session times before you visit.

### Roll into Fun with Roller Skating

When the ice thaws, Codey Arena often converts one of its rinks for roller skating. This is a nostalgic and fun activity for many. Roller skating offers a different kind of challenge and enjoyment. It is a lively atmosphere with music playing. Friends and families often gather here for a fun time.

Similar to ice skating, roller skate rentals are available. This makes it convenient for everyone. Roller skating provides a great workout. It is also a fantastic way to socialize.
