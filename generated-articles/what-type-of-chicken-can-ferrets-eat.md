A common question arises: "What type of chicken can ferrets eat?" Understanding ferret dietary needs is important for their long life and well-being. Ferrets are obligate carnivores, which means meat forms the foundation of their diet. Chicken can be an excellent protein source when prepared correctly. This guide explores the types of chicken suitable for ferrets. We will look at raw chicken benefits, safe cooked options, and crucial safety precautions.
