## The Origins of Pluto: From Rover to Stardom

Then, everything changed in November 1930. He appeared in "The Moose Hunt." This is where he officially became "Pluto the Pup." This name stuck. The timing of his name change is quite interesting. The planet Pluto was discovered in March 1930. Walt Disney and his team likely drew inspiration from this major astronomical event. They wanted a catchy and current name. ### Is Goofy the same as Pluto?

No, Goofy and Pluto are distinctly different characters. Goofy is an anthropomorphic dog. He walks upright, talks, and acts like a human. Pluto, on the other hand, acts purely as a non-speaking pet dog. He displays natural canine behaviors.
