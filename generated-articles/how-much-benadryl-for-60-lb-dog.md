### Avoiding Harmful Ingredients in Benadryl Products

The biggest danger in choosing a Benadryl product for your dog lies in combination medications. Many products sold under the "Benadryl" brand name contain more than just diphenhydramine. These additional ingredients can be very harmful, even deadly, to dogs.

Examples of dangerous ingredients include:

*   **Decongestants:** Pseudoephedrine or phenylephrine are often found in "Benadryl Allergy & Sinus" or similar cold medicines. These can cause heart problems, tremors, and seizures in dogs.
*   **Pain Relievers:** Acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen (Advil) are sometimes combined with antihistamines. Acetaminophen is highly toxic to a dog's liver, and ibuprofen can cause severe stomach ulcers and kidney damage.
*   **Alcohol:** Some liquid formulations contain alcohol as a base. Alcohol is toxic to dogs.

Always read the entire label. Look for "Diphenhydramine HCl" as the ONLY active ingredient. If the label lists other active ingredients, do not use it for your dog.
