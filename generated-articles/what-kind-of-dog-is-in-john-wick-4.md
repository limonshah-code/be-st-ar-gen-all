### Physical Traits and Energy Levels

Belgian Malinois are medium-to-large sized dogs. Males typically weigh between 60-80 pounds and stand 24-26 inches tall. Females are slightly smaller. They possess a muscular build and a sleek, athletic appearance. Their short, weather-resistant coat is usually fawn, tan, or mahogany, often with a black mask and ears.

These dogs have extremely high energy levels. They need a lot of physical activity every day. A simple walk around the block is not enough. They require vigorous exercise, like running, hiking, or dog sports. Without proper exercise, they can become destructive or anxious. Their endurance is truly impressive. Belgian Malinois need extensive daily exercise. This means more than just a short walk. They thrive on activities like running, biking, or intense fetch sessions. Beyond physical exercise, mental stimulation is equally important. These dogs are intelligent and need to use their minds. Puzzle toys, obedience training, agility courses, and scent work are excellent for this. Without mental challenges, they can become bored and destructive. They might chew furniture or dig holes.
