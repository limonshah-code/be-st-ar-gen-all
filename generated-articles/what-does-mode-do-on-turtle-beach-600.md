### Signature Sound: The Balanced Audio Experience

Signature Sound is often the default or first mode on your Turtle Beach Stealth 600. I consider it the "all-rounder" setting. It aims to provide a neutral and balanced audio output. This means no specific frequency range gets a significant boost.

You will hear clear highs, well-defined mid-range tones, and present lows. This mode offers an authentic reproduction of sound. It provides a true representation of the game's original audio mix. Many users appreciate its clarity and natural feel. I often start here when trying a new game.

When should you use Signature Sound? It is perfect for general gaming. It works well for media consumption, like watching movies or listening to music. If you want a reliable, uncolored sound, this mode is your best choice. It ensures you hear everything as it was intended.

### Bass Boost: Feel the Rumble in Your Games

Bass Boost is a favorite among many gamers. This mode amplifies low-frequency sounds. It makes explosions feel more powerful. Vehicle engines sound deeper. You will feel the rumble in your chest, even though it's just audio.

I find this mode incredibly immersive for action-packed games. It truly shines in titles with heavy sound effects or cinematic moments. Think of games with intense firefights or massive environmental destruction. The extra bass adds a layer of excitement. You become more engrossed in the experience.

However, Bass Boost has its trade-offs. Boosting the powerful bass can sometimes muddy other sounds. Dialogue might become less clear. High-frequency details, like distant footsteps, could be harder to pinpoint. It is a fantastic mode for immersion, but perhaps not for competitive play where clarity is key. ### Treble Boost / Bass & Treble Boost: Sharpening Your Hearing

These modes focus on enhancing specific aspects of sound. They are designed to give you an edge or a more dynamic experience. Each brings distinct advantages to your audio.

#### Treble Boost

Treble Boost concentrates on high-frequency sounds. This means sharp noises become more prominent. It is incredibly useful for hearing subtle details. Footsteps, the rustle of leaves, or the distant crack of a sniper rifle become much clearer. I personally use this mode when I need to pinpoint enemy locations. It gives a tactical advantage in stealth-focused games. This mode helps you react faster to environmental cues. It truly sharpens your hearing.

#### Bass & Treble Boost

Bass & Treble Boost aims to deliver a more dynamic sound profile. It combines the powerful lows of Bass Boost with the crisp highs of Treble Boost. This creates a "V-shaped" EQ curve. You get impactful explosions alongside clear details. It offers a more exciting sound without losing crucial information. I find this mode excellent for games that blend action and strategy. It provides a vibrant audio landscape. This mode offers a strong balance for many types of games.

### FAQ Section

#### How do I know which mode is active on my Turtle Beach Stealth 600?

Your headset provides audio cues when you press the mode button. Typically, a specific number of beeps indicates the active mode. For example, one beep might mean Signature Sound, two for Bass Boost, three for Bass & Treble Boost, and four for Superhuman Hearing. Listen carefully as you cycle through the options.

#### Can I create custom EQ presets on the Turtle Beach 600?

No, the Turtle Beach Stealth 600 (both Gen 1 and Gen 2) comes with pre-defined EQ modes. You cannot create custom presets directly on the headset itself. You must choose from the available factory-tuned options. These presets are designed to cover common gaming scenarios effectively.

#### Does "Mode" affect my microphone audio quality?

No, the "Mode" button primarily adjusts the playback audio that you hear through your headset. It does not typically alter your microphone's outgoing audio quality. Microphone monitoring levels, which affect what *you* hear of your own voice, can be adjusted separately but are not tied to the audio modes.

#### Is Superhuman Hearing always the best mode for gaming?

While Superhuman Hearing is excellent for competitive advantages, it is not always the "best" mode. It significantly boosts critical game cues like footsteps. However, it can sometimes make the overall game sound less natural or immersive. Its effectiveness depends heavily on the specific game and your personal preference for sound balance.

#### How do the different Stealth 600 generations handle modes?

Both the original Stealth 600 and the Stealth 600 Gen 2 feature a mode button that cycles through pre-set audio EQs. The core function of adjusting sound profiles remains consistent across generations. The specific names or exact tuning of these presets might vary slightly, but the purpose is the same.

#### Can I use these audio modes when connected to a phone?

Yes, if your Turtle Beach Stealth 600 supports Bluetooth connectivity (like some Gen 2 models), the audio modes should function for audio played through your phone. The modes are processed by the headset's internal hardware. Therefore, they apply to any audio source connected to the headset.
