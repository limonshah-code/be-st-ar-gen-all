### AAFCO Guidelines and Pet Food Regulation

The Association of American Feed Control Officials (AAFCO) sets guidelines for pet food. AAFCO does not regulate pet food directly. Instead, it provides recommendations that states use to create their own pet food laws. AAFCO statements on pet food labels confirm nutritional adequacy. Look for labels that say the food is "complete and balanced."

This statement means the food meets nutrient profiles for a specific life stage. These profiles ensure dogs receive essential nutrients for growth, maintenance, or reproduction. Science Diet prides itself on meeting or exceeding these AAFCO standards. This adherence gives many pet owners peace of mind. Knowing a product meets these benchmarks helps ensure your dog gets proper nutrition.
