To get on Bell Turtle (Walking Mausoleum) in Elden Ring, you must first disable it by destroying the white skull barnacles on its feet or the spirit goo near its base. ## Understanding the Bell Turtles: Walking Mausoleums Explained

The "Bell Turtles" you see in Elden Ring are not living creatures in the traditional sense. They are colossal, mobile structures known officially as Walking Mausoleums. These giants traverse specific areas, often bearing a distinctive bell-like structure beneath them. Their primary purpose is to allow the duplication of Remembrances, powerful items obtained by defeating shardbearers and other major bosses. Each Mausoleum offers a one-time opportunity to duplicate a Remembrance, letting you get a second boss weapon, spell, or item. Knowing how to engage with these structures is key to expanding your arsenal. I quickly learned their value early in my journey.

There are different types of Walking Mausoleums, and their appearance often indicates how you must stop them. Some have large bells underneath their bodies. Others do not. The bell often means a specific way to stop them. Their massive size and slow movement make them intimidating. However, with the right approach, they become a valuable resource for any Tarnished.

*   **Bell-Equipped Mausoleums**: These have a large bell swinging beneath them. You often find them in areas like the Weeping Peninsula or Liurnia of the Lakes. Their design is usually very distinct.
