### True Stories of Heroism: Balto and the Serum Run

One of the most famous sleddog movies is *Balto*, an animated film released in 1995. This movie tells a simplified version of a real historical event. It recounts the 1925 Serum Run to Nome, Alaska. A diphtheria epidemic threatened the children of Nome, a remote town. The only way to deliver life-saving medicine was by dog sled. This urgent mission covered over 600 miles of treacherous terrain. Many brave mushers and their dog teams participated in this relay.

Balto was part of the final leg of this dangerous journey. He led Gunnar Kaasen’s team through a blizzard. Their heroism captured the hearts of people across the United States. The real Balto was a black and white Siberian Husky. He became a symbol of courage and dedication. His statue stands proudly in Central Park, New York City. The movie *Balto* celebrates this incredible feat. It introduces a new generation to the bravery of these sled dogs. It reminds us of the power of unity and determination in times of crisis.
