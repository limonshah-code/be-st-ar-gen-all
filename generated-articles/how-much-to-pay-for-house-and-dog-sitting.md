## FAQ Section

### What is the average daily rate for house and dog sitting?

The average daily rate for house and dog sitting generally ranges from $40 to $100. This includes basic care such as feeding, walks, and companionship. Overnight stays usually cost more, typically between $60 and $120 per night. Prices vary based on location and specific requirements.

### Do I pay more for multiple dogs or pets?

Yes, most house and dog sitters charge an additional fee for extra pets. This is because more animals require more time and attention. Expect to pay an additional $5 to $15 per day for each extra dog or cat. Always confirm these fees upfront with your sitter.

### Should I tip my house and dog sitter?

Tipping is not mandatory, but it is a kind gesture for excellent service. If your sitter went above and beyond, a tip of 15% to 20% of the total bill is appreciated. You can also give a smaller cash tip, a thoughtful gift, or a positive online review.

### What additional services might cost extra?

Additional services that typically incur extra charges include mail collection, plant watering, light housekeeping, and pet transportation. Administering medication or caring for pets with special needs may also have an added fee. Discuss all extra tasks and their costs before booking.

### Is house sitting cheaper than boarding?

House sitting can often be more cost-effective than boarding, especially for multiple pets or longer stays. Boarding kennels charge per pet per night, which adds up quickly. House sitting rates are usually per booking, not per pet, after the initial extra pet fee.

### How do I find a reliable house and dog sitter?

Start by asking friends, family, or your veterinarian for recommendations. Online platforms like Rover, Wag!, or local pet sitting associations also list qualified sitters. Always check references, read reviews, and conduct an in-person interview before hiring.
