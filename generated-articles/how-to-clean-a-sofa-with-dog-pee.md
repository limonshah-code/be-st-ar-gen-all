## Rescue Your Sofa: How To Clean Dog Pee Effectively

I know the feeling. You walk into your living room, and there it is: that unmistakable, unwelcome puddle on your beautiful sofa. A dog pee accident is frustrating for any pet owner, but it does not mean your furniture is ruined. Cleaning a sofa with dog pee requires quick action and the right steps. This guide will help you tackle fresh stains and lingering odors. I will show you how to identify your sofa's fabric, choose the best cleaning method, and remove urine completely. Get ready to restore your sofa to its fresh, clean state.

### Takeaway:

*   Act immediately to blot up fresh urine.
*   Identify your sofa fabric type before cleaning.
*   Use enzymatic cleaners for effective odor and stain removal.
*   Address lingering smells with baking soda and proper ventilation.
*   Consider professional help for stubborn stains or delicate fabrics.

To clean a sofa with dog pee, blot fresh urine immediately with paper towels. Apply an enzymatic cleaner, letting it dwell as directed. Blot the area clean, then rinse with a damp cloth. Finish by air-drying and applying baking soda to absorb residual odors, vacuuming it up later.
