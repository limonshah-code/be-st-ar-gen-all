### The Judging Process

The judging process is very thorough. Dogs first compete within their breed. The best male and female receive awards. Then, one dog is named "Best of Breed." These Best of Breed winners move on to the Group competitions. There are seven distinct groups.

The seven groups are:
*   Hound
*   Toy
*   Non-Sporting
*   Herding
*   Sporting
*   Working
*   Terrier

Each group selects one winner. These seven group winners then compete for Best in Show. The Best in Show judge makes the final choice. They evaluate each dog one last time. This process ensures only the most deserving dog receives the top title. It truly is a rigorous system.
