### The Science Behind Rabies Immunity

Rabies vaccines contain inactivated or weakened forms of the rabies virus. When administered, these components stimulate your pet's immune system. The immune system then produces antibodies that can recognize and neutralize the actual virus. This process creates what we call "active immunity."

This active immunity does not last forever. Over time, the levels of protective antibodies in your pet's body decrease. Booster shots are essential to "remind" the immune system and increase antibody levels back to a protective threshold. Without boosters, your pet's ability to fight off a rabies infection diminishes significantly.
