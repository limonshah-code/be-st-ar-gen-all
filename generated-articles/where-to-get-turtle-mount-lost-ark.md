You need a total of five Island Tokens to acquire the Turtle Mount. These tokens can come from any island. The two crucial tokens are specifically from Whispering Islet and Turtle Island quest completions. You can collect the remaining three from any other accessible islands.

Most mounts in Lost Ark are roster-bound. This means once you acquire the Turtle Mount, all characters on your account (roster) can use it. You only need to complete the acquisition process once. This makes the effort very rewarding for all your alts.
