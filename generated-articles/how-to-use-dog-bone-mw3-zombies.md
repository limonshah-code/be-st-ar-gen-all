## How to Obtain the Dog Bone Schematics and Item

Getting your hands on the Dog Bone, or its schematic, is a primary goal for many MW3 Zombies players. The item itself is consumable, meaning you use it once per deployment. The schematic, however, allows you to craft the Dog Bone repeatedly. This makes the schematic the truly valuable prize. Finding it requires venturing into the game's highest-risk areas.

The most reliable way to find the Dog Bone schematic is by completing objectives within the Dark Aether Rifts. These rifts are endgame content. They feature formidable enemies and challenging contract objectives. Successful completion of these contracts, particularly those in the Elder Sigil version of the Dark Aether, offers the best chance. Reward caches in these zones often contain schematics for powerful items, including the Dog Bone. I remember my first time getting a schematic; it felt like winning the lottery. It took several attempts and careful planning, but the reward was worth it.

Alternatively, you can find the one-time use Dog Bone item. This can sometimes drop from specific reward caches in Tier 3 zones. High-value contracts or boss enemies in these areas might yield the item. These drops are rarer and not guaranteed. This makes the schematic the preferred long-term solution. Once you acquire the schematic, you unlock the ability to craft the Dog Bone. You do this at the aetherium schematic crafting station. This station is typically found near the exfil zones. The crafting process is straightforward once you have the schematic and Essence. Simply interact with the crafting station. Select the Dog Bone from your list of available schematics. Confirm the crafting, and the item appears in your rucksack. Managing your Essence economy is vital for consistent Dog Bone access. Players must balance spending Essence on Pack-A-Punch upgrades and other gear with saving enough to craft items. ## Maintaining Your Hellhound: Health and Revives

Understanding how to maintain your Hellhound's health and manage its revives is crucial. Your Hellhound companion is powerful, but it is not invincible. It has its own health bar. This health bar depletes as zombies and other enemies attack it. Keeping an eye on its status ensures it stays in the fight longer. Ignoring its health can lead to an untimely demise.

When your Hellhound takes too much damage, it will go down. It enters a downed state similar to a player. During this time, it is vulnerable and cannot attack. The good news is that Hellhounds can revive themselves. They will slowly regenerate health and eventually get back up. This self-revive mechanism is a fantastic feature. It prevents you from having to babysit your fiery friend constantly. However, it takes time. In intense firefights, this downtime can be costly. It is important to know that continuous damage while downed can finish it off permanently.

The best way to "heal" your Hellhound is by clearing nearby zombies. Each zombie killed by you or your Hellhound contributes to its healing. This mechanic encourages aggressive play. It rewards you for staying engaged in combat. If your Hellhound is low on health, focus on eliminating threats around it. This indirectly heals it. It gets back into peak fighting condition faster. This also reduces the need for it to enter a downed state. Protecting your Hellhound means protecting yourself. A healthy Hellhound is a powerful asset.

There are certain limitations to consider. The Hellhound's self-revive is not instant. During this period, you lose a valuable combat partner. If you are playing solo, this can leave you exposed. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What exactly does the Dog Bone do in MW3 Zombies?
The Dog Bone in MW3 Zombies summons a Hellhound companion. This Hellhound fights alongside you, attacking zombies and drawing their attention. It can also revive you if you go down during combat. This makes it a crucial item for both offense and defense. It provides a significant combat advantage.

### Q2: Where can I find the Dog Bone schematic?
You can primarily find the Dog Bone schematic by completing high-tier contracts in the Dark Aether Rifts. The Elder Sigil version of the Dark Aether offers the best chances. These are endgame zones with tough enemies. One-time use Dog Bone items can occasionally drop in Tier 3 zones, but the schematic is for crafting.

### Q3: Can I have more than one Hellhound?
Yes, in a squad, multiple players can each deploy a Dog Bone. This results in multiple Hellhounds fighting alongside your team. However, a single player can only have one Hellhound companion summoned at a time. Deploying another Dog Bone will replace your current Hellhound.

### Q4: Does the Hellhound revive itself?
Yes, your Hellhound companion has a self-revive mechanism. If it takes too much damage and goes down, it will slowly recover and get back up. Killing nearby zombies helps speed up its recovery process. It is important to keep clearing threats to maintain its health and uptime.

### Q5: Is the Dog Bone a permanent item?
The Dog Bone itself is a consumable item. You use it once per deployment to summon a Hellhound. However, if you find the Dog Bone *schematic*, you can craft the item repeatedly at the aetherium schematic crafting station. This requires Essence and has a cooldown period after each craft.

### Q6: How long does the Hellhound last?
The Hellhound summoned by the Dog Bone remains active for the duration of your deployment, as long as it does not get permanently eliminated. Its longevity depends on its health and how often it goes down. As long as it can self-revive and you clear zombies to heal it, it will stay with you.
