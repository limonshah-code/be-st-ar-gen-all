### What Drives the Price of Fresh Pet Dog Food?

You probably know that fresh food usually costs more than dry kibble. This is true for human food and also for pet food. Several important factors affect how much Fresh Pet dog food costs. These factors include the quality of ingredients, the way it is made, and how it is kept fresh. Understanding these aspects helps explain the price tag. I find it useful to compare this to human fresh food costs.

#### Premium Ingredients and Production

Fresh Pet uses real, whole ingredients. These include fresh meats, vegetables, and grains. Such ingredients are naturally more expensive than the processed fillers in some dry kibbles. The company avoids artificial preservatives, flavors, and by-products. This commitment to quality impacts the raw material cost. High-quality protein sources like chicken or beef cost more.

The food undergoes gentle cooking. This process helps keep nutrients intact. This method is often more expensive than baking kibble at high temperatures. Specialized equipment is necessary for this type of production. This adds to the manufacturing overhead. All these steps ensure a fresh, nutritious meal for your dog.

#### Refrigeration and Distribution

Fresh Pet food must stay cold. It is a refrigerated product. This means special handling is needed from the factory to the store. The entire supply chain requires refrigeration. This includes trucks, warehouses, and store shelves. Maintaining a cold chain is significantly more expensive than transporting shelf-stable dry kibble.

Grocers must provide refrigerated space for Fresh Pet products. This also adds to the cost. These distribution expenses are built into the final price. The aim is to deliver a truly fresh product. This ensures safety and quality. The fresh format is a key part of the Fresh Pet appeal.

### Average Fresh Pet Costs by Product Type and Size

Fresh Pet offers various product lines. Each line has different forms and sizes. This means prices vary. Knowing these differences helps you budget. I have found specific products fit different needs. Let's look at common options.

#### Fresh Pet Rolls

Fresh Pet rolls are a very popular option. They come in different protein varieties. You slice and serve them. Rolls typically come in 1.5-pound and 6-pound sizes. A 1.5-pound roll might cost around $5 to $8. A larger 6-pound roll could be $15 to $20 or more. The price depends on the specific protein and where you shop.

These rolls are dense and nutritious. Many dogs love the texture and taste. They are easy to store in your refrigerator. You just cut off the amount your dog needs. This makes portion control simple.

#### Fresh Pet Tubs and Meals

Fresh Pet also sells pre-portioned meals in tubs. These are ready to serve. They often come in 1-pound or 2-pound containers. A 1-pound tub might cost $6 to $10. A 2-pound tub could be $10 to $15. These often feature specific recipes or grain-free options.

Tubs are convenient for busy owners. There is less preparation involved. You just open and serve. They are good for smaller dogs or for owners who prefer exact portions. These options provide another way to give fresh food.

#### Smaller Treats and Toppers

Fresh Pet offers smaller items too. These include treats or food toppers. They come in smaller bags or containers. A bag of Fresh Pet treats might cost $4 to $7. These are not full meals. They serve as supplements or special rewards.

These smaller items allow you to introduce fresh ingredients without a full commitment. They can also add variety to a dry kibble diet. Think of them as a fresh addition to your dog's regular routine. They provide a taste of freshness.
