### Common Health Issues in Cairn Terriers and Their Management

Cairn Terriers are generally robust, but like all breeds, they have predispositions to certain health conditions. Being aware of these helps owners provide proactive care. This helps ensure a longer, healthier life. I want every dog owner to know what to look for.

One common issue is **patellar luxation**. This is when the kneecap slips out of place. It can cause limping and pain. Surgery can often correct severe cases. Another concern is **Legg-Calve-Perthes disease**. This condition affects the hip joint. It leads to degeneration of the femur head. Early diagnosis and treatment are important.

**Eye conditions** like cataracts and progressive retinal atrophy can also occur. Regular eye exams can help detect these problems. Skin allergies are also prevalent in Cairn Terriers. These can cause itching and discomfort. Dietary changes or medications can manage allergies.
