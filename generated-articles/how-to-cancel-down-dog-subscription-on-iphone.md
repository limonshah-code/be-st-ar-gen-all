### Step-by-Step Guide to Canceling Down Dog on iPhone (via Settings)

Canceling your Down Dog subscription through your iPhone's Settings app is a straightforward process. This is the most common and recommended method for managing all your Apple subscriptions. I will guide you through each step clearly. Follow these instructions to stop your recurring Down Dog charges.

1.  **Open the Settings App:** Locate the gray gear icon on your iPhone's home screen. Tap it to open your device settings. This app is the central control panel for your iPhone.

2.  **Tap Your Name/Apple ID:** At the very top of the Settings screen, you will see your name and often your profile picture. Tap on this section. This takes you to your Apple ID management page. This page controls all aspects of your Apple account.

3.  **Select "Subscriptions":** On your Apple ID page, scroll down until you find the "Subscriptions" option. It usually has a small icon next to it. Tap "Subscriptions." This action opens a new screen showing all your active and expired subscriptions linked to your Apple ID. This is where you will manage all your app-based recurring payments.

4.  **Find Down Dog:** Look through the list of subscriptions under the "Active" section. You should see "Down Dog" listed here. Tap on the Down Dog entry. If you have many subscriptions, you may need to scroll a bit. The subscriptions are usually listed alphabetically or by purchase date.

5.  **Tap "Cancel Subscription":** On the Down Dog subscription details page, you will see various options. One of them will be "Cancel Subscription." This option is usually highlighted or distinct. Tap it. If you do not see this option, your subscription might have already expired or been canceled. Check the text carefully. It might say "Resubscribe" instead, indicating it is no longer active.

6.  **Confirm Your Cancellation:** A confirmation pop-up will appear. It asks you to confirm your decision to cancel. It also states the date your current subscription period ends. Tap "Confirm" to finalize the cancellation. This is a crucial step. Make sure you fully commit to canceling before confirming.

After these steps, your Down Dog subscription is marked for cancellation. You will receive an on-screen confirmation. Apple also often sends an email notification to your Apple ID's primary email address. This email confirms the cancellation and reiterates the end date of your service. Your access to Down Dog will continue until the specified date.
