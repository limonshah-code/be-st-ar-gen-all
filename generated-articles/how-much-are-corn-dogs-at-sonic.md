### FAQ Section

#### Are Sonic corn dogs always available on the menu?
Yes, Sonic corn dogs are a permanent menu item. You can typically order them any time the drive-in is open. They are a staple product and consistently available across most Sonic locations. This ensures you can satisfy your craving whenever it strikes.

#### Does Sonic have a 50-cent corn dog day often?
Sonic frequently offers 50-cent corn dog days, but not always on a regular schedule. These promotional days happen several times a year. The best way to know when the next one is planned is to check the Sonic app, their website, or their social media channels.

#### Can I customize my Sonic corn dog?
Sonic corn dogs are generally served as-is, meaning you cannot customize the hot dog or the batter itself. However, you can choose from various dipping sauces. You can ask for ketchup, mustard, or other sauces like ranch or BBQ to go with your corn dog.

#### What sauces go well with a Sonic corn dog?
Classic sauces like ketchup and yellow mustard are popular choices for a Sonic corn dog. Some people also enjoy dipping them in honey mustard, barbecue sauce, or even a sweet chili sauce. Sonic offers a range of options, allowing you to pick your favorite.

#### Are Sonic corn dogs vegetarian-friendly?
No, Sonic corn dogs are not vegetarian-friendly. They contain a hot dog, which is made from meat, typically pork and beef. If you are looking for vegetarian options, Sonic offers other items like grilled cheese sandwiches or some sides without meat.

#### Do Sonic corn dog prices vary by time of day?
The standard price of a Sonic corn dog does not usually change by time of day. However, special promotions, like a 50-cent corn dog day, are time-sensitive. These deals make the corn dog much cheaper during the specified promotional period, not a price change on the regular menu item.
