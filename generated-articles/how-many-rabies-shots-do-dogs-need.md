### Takeaway

Protecting your dog from rabies involves understanding specific vaccination steps.
*   **Initial Vaccination:** Puppies receive their first rabies shot between 12 and 16 weeks of age. This initial shot establishes foundational immunity against the rabies virus.
*   **Booster Shots:** The first booster is given one year after the initial vaccination. Subsequent booster shots occur every one or three years, depending on the vaccine used and local laws.
*   **Legal Compliance:** Rabies vaccination is mandatory for dogs in most areas. Always consult your veterinarian and local regulations to ensure your dog meets all requirements.
*   **Lifetime Protection:** Rabies shots offer continuous protection. Following the correct schedule prevents a deadly disease.

The response to a missed shot depends on a few factors. These include how long ago the shot was due and your dog's vaccination history. Local laws also play a critical role here. Some jurisdictions have strict protocols for overdue rabies vaccinations. They might require a specific re-vaccination schedule. They may even impose quarantine periods if your dog has an exposure. It is always best to be proactive. Schedule your dog's vaccinations diligently. ### FAQ Section

#### At what age should a puppy get its first rabies shot?
Puppies typically receive their first rabies vaccination when they are between 12 and 16 weeks old. This timing allows their immune system to respond effectively. It is after any maternal antibodies have diminished. Your veterinarian will confirm the exact best age based on your puppy's health and local regulations.

#### How often do adult dogs need rabies booster shots?
After the initial puppy shot and the one-year booster, adult dogs need rabies booster shots either every one or three years. The frequency depends on the specific vaccine used by your veterinarian and the legal requirements in your area. Always follow your vet's recommendation.

#### What happens if my dog misses a rabies shot?
If your dog misses a rabies shot, contact your veterinarian immediately. They will assess the situation based on how long ago the shot was due and your dog's vaccination history. They might administer the missed booster or restart the vaccination series. Local laws also influence the required action.

#### Is the rabies vaccine mandatory for all dogs?
Yes, rabies vaccination is mandatory for dogs in most states and localities across the United States. These laws are in place to control the spread of rabies and protect both animal and human health. Proof of current rabies vaccination is often required for licensing and travel.

#### Can my dog still get rabies if they are vaccinated?
While rabies vaccines are highly effective, no vaccine provides 100% immunity in every single animal. However, vaccinated dogs are significantly less likely to contract rabies. If a vaccinated dog is exposed, the vaccine greatly reduces the risk of developing the disease. It also often prevents transmission.

#### How much does a rabies vaccine for dogs cost?
The cost of a rabies vaccine for dogs can vary. It usually ranges from $15 to $45, depending on your location, the veterinary clinic, and whether it's part of a broader vaccination package.
