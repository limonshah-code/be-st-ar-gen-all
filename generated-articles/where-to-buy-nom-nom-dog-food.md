## Ensuring Freshness and Storage for Nom Nom Meals

Keeping Nom Nom dog food fresh is key to its benefits. Since it is human-grade and minimally processed, proper storage is important. The company takes steps to ensure freshness upon arrival. You also play a role in maintaining quality at home.

When your Nom Nom delivery arrives, it comes in an insulated box. This box contains dry ice or gel packs. These keep the pre-portioned meals cold during transit. It is vital to unpack the box as soon as possible. Transfer the food to your refrigerator or freezer right away.

The meals are individually sealed. Each pack contains one serving for your dog. This packaging helps maintain freshness. It also makes serving very convenient. You just open a pack and pour.

If you plan to feed the meals within a few days, store them in the refrigerator. Nom Nom meals stay fresh in the fridge for up to seven days. Always check the "use by" date on each package. This ensures your dog eats the food at its best.

For longer storage, you should freeze the meals. Nom Nom food can be stored in the freezer for up to six months. Freezing helps lock in nutrients and freshness. When you are ready to use a frozen meal, move it to the refrigerator to thaw overnight. You can also quickly thaw it in a bowl of cold water. Do not microwave the food in its packaging.

Always serve meals at room temperature or slightly warmed. Your dog might enjoy it more this way. Once a meal is opened, any uneaten portion should be discarded. Do not re-refrigerate or refreeze opened food. This helps prevent bacterial growth.

Proper storage ensures your dog gets the full nutritional value. It also keeps the food safe to eat. Following these simple guidelines helps you maximize the benefits of fresh Nom Nom meals. It is a small effort for a big return in your dog's health.

*   **Immediate Refrigeration/Freezing:** Unpack and store promptly upon arrival.
*   **Refrigerate for Short Term:** Up to 7 days in the fridge.
*   **Freeze for Long Term:** Up to 6 months in the freezer.
*   **Thaw Properly:** Move to fridge overnight or use cold water.
*   **Serve Fresh:** Discard uneaten portions from an opened pack.

Considering the shelf life of fresh food, it makes sense to evaluate how much you store. Unlike dry kibble, fresh food has a limited refrigeration life. This impacts how you manage your inventory.
