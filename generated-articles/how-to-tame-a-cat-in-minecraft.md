### Understanding Cats in Minecraft: Ocelots vs. Stray Cats

Before you can tame a cat, you must find one. Minecraft features two distinct types of feline creatures you can tame. These are the ocelot and the stray cat. Each has unique characteristics and spawn locations. Understanding these differences helps you locate and interact with them effectively. This knowledge is crucial for a successful taming process.

#### Ocelot Spawning and Behavior

Ocelots are shy, wild jungle creatures. They typically spawn in Jungle, Jungle Edge, Bamboo Jungle, and Modified Jungle biomes. You will often see them wandering alone or in small groups. Ocelots are naturally wary of players. If you approach too quickly, they will flee. They do not attack players, but they will chase chickens. This behavior reflects their wild nature. Finding ocelots can sometimes take time due to their evasive movements. You need patience and a careful approach to engage them.

#### Stray Cat Spawning and Behavior

Stray cats are domestic animals found in villages. They also spawn in swamp huts with witches. These cats come in various coat patterns and colors. A maximum of ten cats can exist within a village. They typically spawn near villagers. Unlike ocelots, stray cats do not run away from players. They wander freely through the village. Their calmer demeanor makes them easier to approach. This makes stray cats a good option for first-time cat tamers. They are often less elusive than their jungle counterparts. They naturally deter Creepers from getting too close to villagers.

### The Essential Taming Tool: Raw Fish

Taming a cat in Minecraft requires one specific item: raw fish. You cannot use cooked fish or any other food type for taming. Raw fish, specifically raw cod or raw salmon, are the only items that will work. Having a good supply of this food is important. Taming might take multiple pieces of fish.

#### Obtaining Raw Cod

Raw cod is a common fish found in many water bodies. You can obtain it by fishing with a fishing rod. Any water source block can yield fish. This includes oceans, rivers, and even small man-made ponds. You simply cast your line and wait for the bobber to dip. You can also get raw cod by killing cod fish that swim in oceans and rivers. Cod are abundant in most temperate ocean biomes. They are easy to spot and harvest.

#### Obtaining Raw Salmon

Raw salmon is another excellent choice for taming cats. It is also obtained through fishing. Similar to cod, salmon can be caught in various water biomes. They are more common in colder waters, such as frozen oceans and rivers. You might find them in specific deep ocean biomes too. Killing wild salmon in the water also drops raw salmon. Salmon are generally larger than cod. They provide the same taming value. You do not need to choose between cod and salmon; both work equally well. Just ensure it is raw.

You need to gather enough raw fish. I recommend having at least 5-10 pieces ready. Sometimes, a cat will tame on the first piece. Other times, it requires several attempts. Patience and a good supply ensure success. Gathering fish is usually not difficult. You can often find a good amount within a short time. Having a fishing rod makes the process easier and faster. This prepares you for the actual taming.

### Approaching and Taming Ocelots

Taming an ocelot requires a specific strategy due to their skittish nature. You cannot just run up to them and offer fish. A careful and patient approach is key. Rushing will only scare them away, making the process frustrating. I have learned that slow movements are your best friend here.

#### The Art of Patience

Ocelots are very shy animals. If you sprint or make sudden movements near them, they will run. You must approach them slowly. Hold raw fish in your hand. This will make the ocelot notice you. Then, slowly walk towards it. Crouch by pressing the sneak key. This further reduces your speed and noise. The ocelot might pause and look at you. Do not move when it looks at you. Wait for it to turn its head away. Then, slowly continue your approach. This patient method convinces the ocelot you are not a threat.

#### The Taming Process Step-by-Step

When you are within three blocks of the ocelot, stop moving. Keep the raw fish in your hand. The ocelot should eventually approach you. It will not always come immediately. If it does not, try waiting a moment. Once it is close enough, right-click to feed it the raw fish. Hearts will appear around the ocelot if the taming is successful. If hearts do not appear, try feeding it another piece of fish. You might need to feed several pieces before it tames.

Once tamed, the ocelot transforms into a random cat variant. It will wear a collar. You can tell it's tamed by this collar and its new behavior. It will follow you and become your pet. Remember, ocelots will not instantly turn into cats every time you feed them. It is a chance-based system. Just keep feeding until it works. ### Taming Stray Cats in Villages

Taming a stray cat is generally easier than taming an ocelot. Stray cats are found in villages and swamp huts. They are already somewhat accustomed to humanoid presence. This makes their taming process more straightforward. I usually find these cats to be less skittish.

#### Locating Villages

Villages are common structures found in many biomes. You can find them in plains, deserts, savannas, taigas, and snowy plains. Cats naturally spawn within these village boundaries. They wander around the buildings and near villagers. You might see several cats in one village. Swamp huts also have one black cat living with a witch. This is another specific location to find a stray cat. Exploring new chunks often leads to discovering new villages. Marking village locations on your map helps if you plan to get multiple cats.

#### Taming Village Cats

Once you find a stray cat, the taming process is simple. Hold raw cod or raw salmon in your hand. Approach the cat. Unlike ocelots, you do not need to sneak or worry as much about scaring them. Just walk up to the cat. When you are close, right-click to feed it the fish. Hearts will appear if the taming is successful. The cat will then gain a collar and follow you. It will also adopt a specific coat pattern. There are eleven different coat patterns for stray cats. This makes each one unique.

You might need to feed a stray cat more than one piece of fish. The taming process for stray cats is also chance-based. However, it often feels quicker due to their calmer demeanor. ### Caring for Your Tamed Minecraft Cat

Once you have successfully tamed a cat, ensuring its well-being is important. Tamed cats are not invincible. They can take damage and get lost. Proper care ensures your feline companion stays healthy and safe. I always make sure to keep an eye on my pets.

#### Healing Your Cat

Tamed cats have health points, just like players. They can get hurt by falls, hostile mobs, or even other players. If your cat takes damage, its health bar will decrease. You can heal your tamed cat by feeding it raw fish. Raw cod or raw salmon will restore a small amount of their health. Each piece of fish restores 2 health points (1 heart). It is a good idea to carry some extra raw fish. This allows you to quickly heal your cat if it gets injured. Keeping your cat healthy ensures it can continue to provide its benefits.

#### Breeding Cats

You can breed two tamed cats to create a kitten. To do this, you need two tamed cats. You also need raw fish (cod or salmon) for each cat. Feed one raw fish to the first cat. Then, feed one raw fish to the second cat. Hearts will appear, indicating they are in "love mode." A kitten will then spawn. The kitten will automatically be tamed and have a collar. It will also inherit the coat pattern of one of its parents. This is a great way to grow your cat collection. Breeding also ensures you have more Creeper and Phantom deterrents.

#### Preventing Despawning

Tamed cats do not despawn naturally. This means they will not disappear on their own. However, they can get lost if you wander too far away. They can also die from various hazards. To prevent losing your cat, you can keep it close to your base. Placing them in a safe area, like inside your house, is a good idea. You can command them to sit by right-clicking them. A sitting cat will not follow you. This keeps them in a specific spot. This is useful if you are going on a dangerous adventure. Always remember where you left them. ### FAQ Section

#### How do you find cats in Minecraft?
You find cats in Minecraft in two main ways. Ocelots spawn naturally in Jungle biomes, including Bamboo Jungles. Stray cats spawn within villages. They also spawn in swamp huts, living with witches. Look for these specific locations when you are searching for a feline friend.

#### What food do you use to tame a cat in Minecraft?
You use raw fish to tame a cat in Minecraft. Specifically, raw cod and raw salmon are the only items that work. Cooked fish or any other food item will not initiate the taming process. Make sure you have a good supply of raw fish before approaching a cat.

#### Can tamed cats teleport in Minecraft?
Yes, tamed cats can teleport in Minecraft. If you move more than 12 blocks away from your sitting cat, it will teleport to you. This also happens if you travel to a different dimension, like the Nether or the End. They will not teleport if you are in a minecart or boat.

#### Do cats protect you from Creepers in Minecraft?
Yes, tamed cats protect you from Creepers in Minecraft. Creepers will flee if a tamed cat is within a 6-block radius of them. This makes cats excellent passive guards for your base or during exploration. They help prevent unwanted explosions and damage.

#### How do you heal a cat in Minecraft?
You heal a cat in Minecraft by feeding it raw fish. Raw cod or raw salmon will restore your cat's health. Each piece of raw fish provides 2 health points (1 heart). Always carry extra raw fish to keep your feline companion healthy during adventures.

#### Can you breed cats in Minecraft?
Yes, you can breed cats in Minecraft. You need two tamed cats and two raw fish. Feed one raw fish to each cat. They will enter love mode, and a kitten will spawn. The kitten will automatically be tamed and wear a collar.
