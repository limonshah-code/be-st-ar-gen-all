### Key Aspects of Border Collie Behavior

Understanding their behavior helps potential owners know what to expect. It also helps us appreciate Hurricane's natural abilities even more. These dogs are not for everyone, but they are incredibly rewarding for the right person.

*   **Intense and Energetic:** Border Collies are high-energy dogs. They need at least one to two hours of vigorous exercise daily. This is not just a walk around the block; it means running, fetch, or dog sports. Without enough activity, they can become restless or destructive.
*   **Sensitive and Responsive:** They are highly attuned to their owner's moods and commands. This sensitivity makes them very responsive during training. However, it also means they do not respond well to harsh training methods. Positive reinforcement works best.
*   **Reserved with Strangers:** Many Border Collies are initially reserved or wary of new people. They are loyal to their family. Proper socialization from a young age helps them become more comfortable in new situations.
*   **Prone to Herding Instincts:** Even without sheep, their herding instinct is strong. They might try to "herd" children, other pets, or even moving cars. This is a natural behavior that owners need to manage with training.
