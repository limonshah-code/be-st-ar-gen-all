### Takeaway

Here are the key points about Grey French Bulldog size:

*   Grey French Bulldogs follow the standard French Bulldog size range. Their coat color does not change their physical dimensions.
*   Adult Grey French Bulldogs typically stand 11-13 inches tall. They weigh between 16-28 pounds.
*   Males are often slightly larger and heavier than females.
*   Factors like genetics, diet, and exercise significantly impact their final size and healthy weight.
*   Most Grey French Bulldogs reach their full size by 9-12 months of age.

Grey French Bulldogs are compact, sturdy dogs. They generally weigh 16-28 pounds and stand 11-13 inches tall at the shoulder. Their "grey" coat color does not affect their size. These dogs mature to their full adult dimensions around 9 to 12 months old.
