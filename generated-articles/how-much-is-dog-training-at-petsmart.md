## Adult Dog Training Programs and Their Costs

Once your dog moves past the puppy stage, their training needs evolve. PetSmart offers several levels of adult dog training. These programs build on basic skills or address more specific behaviors. They help owners develop a stronger bond with their adult dogs. The adult programs are typically structured into Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced Obedience classes.

### Beginner Obedience

The Beginner Obedience class is often the starting point for adult dogs. It costs approximately $129 to $149. This six-week program teaches core commands. Dogs learn sit, stay, come, and loose-leash walking. Trainers also cover problem behaviors like jumping and barking. This class is great for dogs new to formal training or those needing a refresher.

### Intermediate Obedience

After mastering beginner skills, dogs can move to Intermediate Obedience. This course usually costs around $159 to $179. It focuses on improving consistency and reliability of commands. Dogs learn to follow commands with distractions. They also work on more complex behaviors. This class strengthens the dog's focus and impulse control.

### Advanced Obedience and Beyond

For those who want to push further, Advanced Obedience is available. This class can cost from $199 to $279. It prepares dogs for more complex tasks and public good manners. Some PetSmart locations also offer specialized classes. These might include tricks or Canine Good Citizen preparation. These advanced courses help dogs become exemplary companions in any setting. Each level builds confidence and improves communication between you and your dog.
