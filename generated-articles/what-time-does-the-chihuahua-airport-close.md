## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Chihuahua Airport open 24/7?

### What are the busiest times at CJS?
Busiest times at Chihuahua Airport typically occur during morning and late afternoon/early evening hours. These periods align with peak departure and arrival schedules. Holiday seasons and long weekends also bring increased passenger traffic. Expect longer lines and more activity during these times, making early arrival important.

### Are there hotels near Chihuahua Airport?
Yes, several hotels are located near Chihuahua International Airport (CJS). These accommodations cater to travelers with early flights or late arrivals. ### What food options are available late at night?
