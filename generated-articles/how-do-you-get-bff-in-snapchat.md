#### The Yellow Heart: Your Best Friend

The yellow heart emoji signifies that you and another user are "Besties." This means you are each other's #1 best friend on Snapchat. You send more snaps to this person, and they send more snaps to you, than any other friend. This is a significant milestone, as it means you are mutually at the top of each other's friend lists. To get the yellow heart, you must exchange the most snaps with a specific friend over a consistent period. This interaction must be more frequent than with anyone else on your friends list. #### The Red Heart: Your True BFF

The red heart emoji means you and another user have been "Besties" (yellow heart) for at least two consecutive weeks. This is the official "BFF" status. Achieving the red heart shows sustained, intense interaction over a longer period. It indicates a consistent and exclusive top friendship on Snapchat. This emoji truly symbolizes your closest bond on the platform.

Once you have the yellow heart, maintaining that #1 best friend status for two weeks straight will upgrade it to a red heart. This requires continued, consistent snapping back and forth. You both must ensure your interaction remains higher with each other than with anyone else. The red heart is a clear sign of a strong and enduring Snapchat friendship. ### FAQs About Snapchat BFF Status

#### How long does it take to get BFF on Snapchat?

It typically takes at least two consecutive weeks of being "Besties" (yellow heart emoji) to achieve BFF status (red heart emoji). You must first be each other's number one best friend, exchanging the most snaps with that person. This consistent, mutual interaction over two weeks leads to the upgrade. So, a minimum of two weeks after reaching "Besties" is required.

#### Can you lose BFF status on Snapchat?

Yes, you can lose BFF status. If your interaction with your red-heart BFF declines, or if you start interacting more with someone else, the red heart can revert to a yellow heart or even disappear. Maintaining BFF status requires ongoing, consistent, and mutual top-tier interaction. It's an active status that needs continuous effort.

#### Does chatting count towards BFF status?

Yes, direct chat messages, including text, images, and videos sent within the chat, contribute to your overall interaction score. While sending individual photo/video snaps is generally given more weight, consistent chatting helps strengthen your friendship ranking. It shows a deeper, multi-faceted communication pattern. Utilizing chat alongside snaps is beneficial.

#### What's the difference between Besties and BFF on Snapchat?

"Besties" is indicated by a yellow heart emoji and means you are each other's #1 best friend. You send and receive the most snaps with this person. "BFF" is indicated by a red heart emoji and means you have been "Besties" (yellow heart) for at least two consecutive weeks. BFF signifies a longer-term, more established top friendship.

#### Do group snaps help you get BFF status?

Group snaps contribute to your overall Snap Score and general activity on Snapchat. However, for achieving BFF status with a specific individual, one-on-one snaps are far more impactful. The algorithm prioritizes direct, individual interactions to determine your closest friend. Focus on sending individual snaps to your target BFF.

#### Is there a secret trick to get BFF status faster?

No, there is no secret trick or shortcut to get BFF status faster. The process relies on genuine and consistent interaction. The algorithm is designed to identify true, active best friends through sustained engagement. Focus on daily, mutual individual snapping, chatting, and calling. Authenticity and consistency are the only "tricks."
