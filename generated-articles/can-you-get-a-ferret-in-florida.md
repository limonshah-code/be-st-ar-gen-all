## Understanding Florida's Ferret Laws and FWC Regulations

Florida has strict rules about exotic pet ownership. The state aims to protect its delicate ecosystem. These regulations also ensure public safety. ### The FWC's Classification of Ferrets

The FWC classifies ferrets (Mustela putorius furo) as Class III wildlife. This classification means they are not considered domestic animals like cats or dogs. Instead, the FWC views them as potentially dangerous or harmful to native wildlife. Owning a Class III animal requires a special permit. This permit is not easily granted to individuals for simple pet ownership. My research shows the FWC considers factors like species aggression and disease risk. They also weigh the potential impact on native habitats.
