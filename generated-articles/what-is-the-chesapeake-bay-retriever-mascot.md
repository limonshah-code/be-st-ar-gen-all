This breed originated from two Newfoundland dogs rescued from a ship wreck in 1807. Local Maryland breeders crossed these dogs with other retrievers and hounds. They aimed to create a robust waterfowl retriever.

The resulting dogs were truly special. They could withstand harsh weather and retrieve hundreds of ducks in a single day. This history explains their thick, oily coat and powerful build. The Chesapeake Bay Retriever quickly became a beloved working dog in the region.
