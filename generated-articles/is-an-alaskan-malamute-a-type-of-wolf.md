### Q2: Are Alaskan Malamutes aggressive like wolves?
No, Alaskan Malamutes are not aggressive like wolves. They are affectionate and loyal. They are not naturally aggressive towards humans. They can be strong-willed. ### Q3: How much exercise does an Alaskan Malamute need?
Alaskan Malamutes require a lot of exercise. They are high-energy working dogs. They need at least 1-2 hours of vigorous activity daily. This can include running, hiking, or sledding. ### Q4: Can an Alaskan Malamute live with other pets?
Alaskan Malamutes can live with other pets. Early socialization is key. They have a strong prey drive. This drive can make them challenging with smaller animals like cats or rabbits. They generally do well with other dogs. Always introduce new pets carefully and supervise interactions.

### Q5: What is the average lifespan of an Alaskan Malamute?
The average lifespan of an Alaskan Malamute is typically 10 to 14 years. This lifespan is comparable to many large dog breeds. Proper nutrition, regular exercise, and routine veterinary care contribute to a long and healthy life. Responsible breeding also helps reduce genetic health issues.

### Q6: Do Alaskan Malamutes howl or bark more?
Alaskan Malamutes are known for their distinctive "woo-woo" sound. They also howl. They tend to howl more than bark. Their vocalizations are part of their communication. This can be a charming trait. However, it can also be quite loud. It is important for owners to understand their vocal nature.
